# Leave entitlement overview (Hong Kong)

Orientation map for **Hong Kong-facing** clipped pages. Canonical enumerations: **[[wiki/sources/Leave Entitlement]]** (+ specialist pages).

## Snapshot (from entitlement table digest)

| Area | Highlights (per clip) |
|------|------------------------|
| PTO | 20 days first 10 years service, 25 thereafter — booking rules & minimums in [[wiki/concepts/Paid time off policy (Workday)]] |
| Public holidays | Year-specific list — [[wiki/concepts/Public holidays (Hong Kong)]] |
| Floating leave | 2 days / year |
| Unpaid | After PTO exhausted |
| TOIL | Travel-driven — [[wiki/concepts/Time off in lieu for business travel]] |
| Personal / extended LOA | **See contradiction note below** |
| Sabbatical | 30 business days paid |
| Sick | 2 days/mo year 1 then 4/mo, cap 120 days |
| Maternity | Table references 40 weeks etc. — verify statutory vs policy text in raw |
| Enhanced bonding | 12 weeks @ 100% base — [[wiki/concepts/Enhanced bonding leave]] |
| Caregiving | 12 weeks rolling 12 months @ 50% base |
| Compassionate | 10 days / event — [[wiki/concepts/Global compassionate leave]] |
| Volunteering | 3 days / calendar year — [[wiki/sources/Bright Days Off]] |

**HK sick certificate (ticket evidence):** a resolved **People & Purpose** reply on [[wiki/sources/Sick leave enquiry]] states **no sick certificate** for **1 day** paid sick leave and a certificate when leave runs **more than two days** — **informal case thread**; reconcile with Compass / published entitlement text if they differ.

## Contradictions

- **Personal & Extended LOA row** in [[wiki/sources/Leave Entitlement]] understates **day-one 90-day PLOA** detailed in [[wiki/sources/Personal and Extended Leave of Absence]]. **Trust the PLOA page + concept** when planning short unpaid leave.
