# Sales compensation plan FY2027 (Workday)

FY2027 **Sales Compensation Plan** pairs **General Terms** (global administrative rules) with a **Individual Goal Sheet** per participant (quotas, splits, tier rates). Canonical period and mechanics: [[wiki/sources/FY27 General Terms]].

## Participant package

| Piece | Role |
|-------|------|
| [[wiki/sources/FY27 General Terms]] | Eligibility, Plan Period, attainment/commission frameworks, LOA, termination, governance, Seismic incorporation |
| [[wiki/sources/FY2027 Individual Goal Sheet (PlanDocument)]] | Participant instance: territory weightings, quota grid, tier rates, crediting/payment schedule (figures stay in [[raw/PlanDocument.pdf]]) |

## Interfaces with HR leave concepts

Sales Plan **Qualified vs Non-Qualified** leave and **26-week** windows interact with **People & Purpose** definitions — see LOA section in [[wiki/sources/FY27 General Terms]]. Do not assume HR [[wiki/concepts/Leave entitlement overview (Hong Kong)]] labels map 1:1 to **Qualified Leave** for commission; use **People Guide Request** when unclear.
