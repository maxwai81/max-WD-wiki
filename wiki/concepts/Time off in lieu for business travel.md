# Time off in lieu for business travel

Mechanics on [[wiki/sources/Time Off in Lieu]]: TOIL accrual for **weekend/public holiday business travel** with manager pre-approval, **0.5 vs 1 day** rules, **6-month** usage window, **non-cashable** on exit, soft cap ~**8 days/year** expectation.

Relates to [[wiki/concepts/Business travel assistance (ISOS and Chubb)]] for travel risk context.
