# Corporate travel and expense card (Workday)

**What it is:** Workday’s **Corporate Travel & Expense (T&E)** credit card programme, issued/administered through **Bank of America (BAML)**. Use this concept when pairing **policy obligations** with **activation, PIN, alerts, disputes,** and threshold rules.
