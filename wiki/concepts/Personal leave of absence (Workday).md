# Personal leave of absence (Workday)

Unpaid discretionary leave described on [[wiki/sources/Personal and Extended Leave of Absence]].

## Essentials

| Mode | Duration | Typical gate |
|------|----------|---------------|
| PLOA | ≤90 calendar days | Day-one eligible populations (FT regular + interns globally per clip) |
| Extended | 91 days – ≤12 months | ≥5 yrs service (FR **3 yrs** caveat), perf standing, spacing rules |

Booking + approvals escalate to SVP (+ CHRO extended). Economics: unpaid cash comp stops; vested equity rules + benefits continuation caveats articulated in departure letter bullets.

Always compare against [[wiki/concepts/Leave entitlement overview (Hong Kong)]] narrative.
