# Paid time off policy (Workday)

Compiled from the **regional** PTO policy clipped at the HK benefits site. **HK calendar entitlement** (e.g. days per service band, local carry-over deadlines) lives in [[wiki/sources/Leave Entitlement]] and linked **annual leave** portal pages; this page captures **process and guardrails** that apply alongside those numbers.

## Minimum usage and timing

- **20 days** PTO minimum per **calendar year**; **10 days** annual leave by **30 September**.
- Booking **lead times:** 2 weeks (≤3 working days off), 4 weeks (longer blocks); emergencies exception with approval.
- Approval only when **confirmed in Workday**.

## Balance management

- Org may push scheduling to meet minimums or reduce **high** balances.
- **Audits** if balance discrepancy suspected; cooperation expected.
- **Forfeiture** of unused accrual possible (**AU/NZ** carve-out for local law).
- **Encashment** may wait on audit outcome (see local entitlement rules).

## Carry-over

- **5 days** standard; statutory or approved business case for more; **PBP-documented** approval for extended carry-over; **local statute** prevails where it differs.

## Other mechanics

- **Notice period:** generally **exhaust** PTO; manager **discretion** on timing.
- **Sick during PTO:** notify leader; with sick documentation, convert to **sick leave**.
- **Blackout/peak** periods: possible **team caps** and advance communication.
- **Annual leave adjacent to sick leave** without documentation may be **reviewed**; abuse path to **P&P investigation**.
- Breaches may mean **disciplinary** action.

## Contradictions

- None flagged vs other wiki pages; **pair** this hub with [[wiki/sources/Leave Entitlement]] for HK row-level entitlements (e.g. 25 days after 10 years) — the regional policy emphasises a **floor** of 20 days, not the full HK ladder.
