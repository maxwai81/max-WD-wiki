# Caregiving leave (Workday)

## Essentials

- **Duration:** up to **12 weeks** within any rolling **12-month** window
- **Pay:** **50% of base salary** during leave
- **Trigger:** caring for an ill or dependent loved one (child, family member)

Source: [[wiki/sources/Leave Entitlement]] (master leave table).

## Relationship to other leave types

| Leave | Pay | Duration | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caregiving | 50% base | ≤12 wks / 12 months | Ongoing care of ill dependent |
| Compassionate | 100% | ≤10 days / event | Acute, unanticipated event |
| PLOA | Unpaid | ≤90 days (extended ≤12 months) | Longer structural absence |

Layer caregiving leave first; use [[wiki/concepts/Global compassionate leave]] for the initial acute period, then transition to caregiving leave for sustained care needs.
