# Life insurance (AIA Hong Kong)

Employer-arranged group insurance via **AIA HK**. Full digest: [[wiki/sources/Life Insurance]].

## Coverage stack

| Cover | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Group term life | 4× Basic Annual Salary | Cap HKD 6,000,000 |
| AD&D | 4× BAS | Cap HKD 8,000,000 |
| TPD | 4× BAS | Cap HKD 6,000,000 |
| Critical Illness | HKD 500,000 | 60-day waiting period |
| LTD | 70% of basic salary | 90-day wait; max 5-year payment period |

## Eligibility

- **Permanent employees** ≥ 15 hrs/week, from hire date
- Casuals and contractors excluded
- Coverage age: entry to **64**, renewable to **70**
- Workday system visibility from **1 April** — hires before that date are still covered from actual hire date
