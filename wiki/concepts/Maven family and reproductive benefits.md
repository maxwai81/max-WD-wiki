# Maven family and reproductive benefits

Integrated virtual care + wallet reimbursements described across [[wiki/sources/Women's Health and Family Support]], [[wiki/sources/Here to Support Your Health]], and Maven callouts in [[wiki/sources/Taking Time Off 2]].

**Maven Wallet** reimbursements are **taxable** (multiple reminders). Lifetime caps **location-specific** messaging.

> **HK employees: Maven Wallet is NOT available as of 2026-02-01.** Maven Wallet (fertility/adoption/surrogacy reimbursement) was restricted to **US employees only** effective the 2026-02-01 policy refresh. HK Workmates should rely solely on AIA Plan 001 (HKD 50,000/year fertility limit) for insurance-backed fertility funding. Maven core virtual care (coaching, clinical programmes) may still apply — confirm scope with P&P.

Align clinical spend sequencing with [[wiki/concepts/Group medical insurance (AIA Hong Kong)]] when planning fertility treatment.
