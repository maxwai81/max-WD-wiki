## Summary

Operational **how-to** for Workday **Corporate Travel & Expense Credit Card**: requests (new card / limit change / cancellation / lost or stolen → Workday task link in raw), **activation** by phone with region-dependent questions, optional **verification / activation ID** for US/Canada/APAC (**“9” + Workday employee ID**, e.g. ID 392 → 9392), and manual **BAML associate** fallback (examples given: Password/Workday ID, home address).

**PIN:** one-time registration at **[www.bofaml.com/globalcardaccess](https://www.bofaml.com/globalcardaccess)** (“Register a card”); typical fields listed in raw include 16-digit number, **name as on card (CAPS)**, credit limit (no commas/decimals/currency symbols), CVV, DOB (**DD/MM/YYYY** format noted). Raw notes PIN may need **2–3 attempts** before it authenticates.

**Alerts:** Global Card Access → **alerts / contacts**; recommended types in clip: **Suspicious Activity**, **Transactions Declined**, **Percentage of Credit Limit Used**.

**When to use the card (threshold):** per linked **Travel and Expense Policy** in raw — after **$3,000 USD** (or **local currency equivalent**) in **annual travel expenses**, Workmates **must request and use** the corporate card for **future travel-related spend** — authoritative policy wording and effective dates live in that policy (**not clipped in vault**).

**Eligible spend examples:** airfare, hotel, ride-hail while traveling, travel meals, parking fees, etc.

**Generally exclude or escalate:** cellphone bills charged to the corporate card; **large purchases over $1,000 USD** (or equivalent) better routed via **purchase order**; **personal/non-business** items. If unsure → **expenses@workday.com**.

**Fraud / disputes:**

- Contact **Bank of America fraud** — **North America:** (866) 500-8262 or card-back number; **EMEA/APAC:** (877) 436-0960 or card-back number.
- **Within 30 days** of incurring the charge (per FAQ): BoA handles pursuit of reimbursement and may replace card if fraudulent — **also** submit the transaction in Workday under expense item **“Fraud/Disputed Transaction.”**
- **After 30 days:** email Expense (**expenses@workday.com**, subject **“Fraudulent Credit Card Activity”**), try vendor refund, involve manager if no refund; still report in Workday as **“Fraud/Disputed Transaction.”**
- **If vendor refunds:** still submit under **“Fraud/Disputed Transaction”** per FAQ numbering.
