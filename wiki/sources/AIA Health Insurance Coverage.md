# AIA Health Insurance Coverage — Benefit Summary (Plan 001)

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/AIA Health Insurance Coverage.pdf]]  
**Document:** “Benefit Summary”, marked **[AIA - CONFIDENTIAL]** — **Plan 001**.  
**Benefit period (footer):** effective **2026-02-01** to **2027-01-31** — aligns with Feb–Jan cycle on [[wiki/sources/Health Insurance 1]].  
**Currency:** all figures **HKD/MOP** per document.

## Summary

Structured **benefit schedule** (in-network vs non-network columns) for hospitalization, outpatient, dental, maternity, fertility, evacuation/repatriation, vision, check-up, and China Assist. Use as **quantitative anchor** alongside portal narrative on [[wiki/sources/Health Insurance 1]].

## Claims (high-signal figures)

- **Overall maximum** per insured person per policy year: **HKD 10,000,000** (table header — applies broadly except where remarks carve out evacuation/repatriation).
- **Hospitalization:** **private** room & board; **100%** reimbursement % (in-network column); **US admission — pre-approval required** or benefit not payable.
- **Outpatient:** GP / tele-GP **100% up to HKD 1,000/visit** ($0 copay in-network); specialist / tele-specialist **100% up to HKD 2,000/visit**; physio **100% up to HKD 1,000/visit**, max **30 visits/year**; prescribed medicine (≤60 days, prescription required) non-network **100% up to HKD 5,000/day**; psychiatry/psychology OP **100% of actual charges**, max **20 visits/year**; **overall OP maximum HKD 780,000** per insured per policy year.
- **Medical checkup** (employees, spouses, partners & children **over 18**): non-network **100% up to HKD 10,500/policy year**.
- **Vision:** eye exam max **1/year**, **100% up to HKD 2,000/policy year**; lenses/frames/sunglasses as described in schedule.
- **Dental:** three classes (investigative/preventative **100%**; basic restorative etc. **80%**; major **50%**); orthodontics **children only**, **50% up to HKD 9,360/policy year**; **overall dental maximum HKD 10,000**/year.
- **Maternity:** miscarriage/abortion medical necessity **100% actual**; normal/C-section, complications, pre/postnatal (**6 weeks** postnatal in clip) as summarized — **no waiting period** on delivery mode per remark **13**; elective C-section included per remark.
- **Fertility treatment:** non-network **100% up to HKD 50,000 per policy year**; remark **19** lists covered modalities (IVF, ICSI, GIFT, ZIFT, AI/IUI, cryopreservation, embryo transport, donor gamete costs, elective egg freeze for delayed pregnancy, etc.) and **exclusions** (unlisted treatments, sterilisation reversal/management tied to failed infertility treatment, **expenses once pregnant**).
- **China Assist:** up to **HKD 10,000 per hospitalization**; remark **10** describes **deposit guarantee up to HKD/MOP 10,000** at selected mainland hospitals with China Assist Card, travel ≤ **90 days**.
- **Evacuation & repatriation:** emergency evacuation **100%**; repatriation of remains **100%**; worldwide hospitalization deposit guarantee **max HKD 60,000/trip**; compassionate visit, return of children, overseas monitoring, convalescence hotel caps per schedule (**visitor accommodation max HKD 12,000/trip**, convalescence **HKD 2,000/day** max **5 days**).

## Administrative / interpretation

- Remark **5:** all benefits subject to **overall maximum** **except** medical evacuation & repatriation benefit (special handling).
- Referral rules, telemedicine exclusions (e.g. **no medication delivery** charge coverage), **waived referral** items for certain specialties (remarks **6–9**).
- **VIP/Deluxe** hospital accommodation **not payable** (remark **1**).
- Final interpretation → **master policy** issued by **AIA International Limited** (Bermuda).
