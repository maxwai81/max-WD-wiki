# Enhanced Bonding Leave — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/Enhanced Bonding Leave]]  
**Source URL:** `https://benefits.workday.com/hk/enhanced-bonding-leave`

## Summary

Globally marketed **Enhanced Bonding Leave:** up to **12 weeks paid at 100% base salary** for **birth or adoption**, subject to **local law** where that exceeds 12 weeks (greater of). **Enhanced pay is inclusive of statutory** entitlements. Eligible from **first day** as employee **if child born/adopted after hire date**.

## Claims

- **UK and Ireland caveat:** **public holidays do not accrue** under this leave type (explicit in clip).
- **Workbaby kits** sent automatically after parental leave workflow, **8–12 weeks** after submitting leave request unless opted out via People Guide Request; gift **tax implications** noted (Workday withholding where required).

## Contradictions

- [[wiki/concepts/Leave entitlement overview (Hong Kong)]] cites **Enhanced Bonding 12 weeks** — aligned.
- Clip title says “Parental Leave” synonym in key takeaways; statutory **maternity** details for HK appear on [[wiki/concepts/Leave entitlement overview (Hong Kong)]] via Leave Entitlement table (40 weeks wording etc.) — do not confuse statutory maternity row with Enhanced Bonding row.
