# Life Insurance — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/Life Insurance]]  
**Source URL:** `https://benefits.workday.com/hk/life-insurance`

## Summary

Employer-arranged coverage with **AIA HK** (clip also says **Zurich partner** phrasing alongside AIA — treat as positioning; **AIA HK** appears as insurer). Covers **group term life, AD&D, TPD, Critical Illness, LTD** bundles for **permanent employees** ≥**15 hrs/week**, **from hire** (casuals/contractors out).

## Claims (sums / structure)

- **Life & TPD**: **HKD 6,000,000** stated cap line in clip; **AD&D HKD 8,000,000**; CI **HKD 500,000** (**60-day** CI waiting period noted); LTD **70%** of basic salary, **90-day** wait, **max 5 years** payment period per table narrative.
- Volume formula: Life/AD&D/TPD at **4× Basic Annual Salary (BAS)** in table excerpt.
- **Age:** entry coverage to **64**, renew to **70** mentioned.
- **Workday visibility** from **1 April** with note that hires before then still covered from **actual hire date** for true coverage commencement.
