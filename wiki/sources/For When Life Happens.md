# For When Life Happens — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/For When Life Happens]]  
**Source URL:** `https://benefits.workday.com/hk/for-when-life-happens`

## Summary

**Narrative hub** (not a standalone entitlement table) pointing to coping resources: bereavement (**compassionate leave** + counselling), **divorce/separation** (time off + counselling + financial guidance), **moving house** (financial advisor + leave options), **long illness** (**salary continuation** / extended leave — see local **leave entitlement**), **caregiving for ill loved ones** (**compassionate leave**), **financial change** (**Northstar**).

## Claims

- Framing emphasises counselling and financial advising routes **in addition** to formal leave programmes.

## Contradictions

- **Potential tension:** prose suggests salary continuation broadly for extended illness vs detailed math on [[wiki/concepts/Leave entitlement overview (Hong Kong)]] — always anchor numbers to entitlement/sick leave pages.
