## Summary

**Mind Matters**, powered by **Optum**, is global **mental health / EAP** offering: portal **supportfinder.optum.com/workday**, **24/7 assistance line**, **unlimited coaching** + **up to 8 short-term counselling sessions per issue per year** framing (therapy/counselling count), couples pathway via Workplace Mental Health Consultants (WMHC) per clip. **Availability subject to local law.**

## Workplace Mental Health Consultants (WMHC)

- Clip lists **booking Calendly/Outlook links** segmented by geography (**EMEA, UK, Dublin, APAC Sydney** etc.) — **HK Workmates travelling or virtual** routes may vary; onsite care **active employees**.
- Confidentiality emphasized; employer typically **does not receive usage detail** vs carve-outs (safety/legal).

## Limits / exclusions FAQ

- Not long-term therapy, inpatient, legal representation fees, debt settlement payouts, medical psychiatry substitution per “what EWS does not cover” section.
