## Summary

Binding **Corporate Travel and Expense (T&E) Card** user agreement framing: read together with **Travel and Expense Policy**; issued after **review and acknowledgement** saves out-of-pocket + reimbursement friction.

**Numbered clauses (mirrors raw items 1–15 + trailing Amazon subsection):**

1. Read together with **Travel and Expense Policy**.
2. Cards **business purposes only**.
3. **All** **business-related travel expenses** on T&E Cards.
4. Cards used **only by** the named holder (**T&E Cardholder**).
5. Holder **must** submit **expense reports** plus required docs for **all charges** within **30 days** of transaction date.
6. Non-compliance may bring **card suspension/revocation** and discipline **up to and including termination**.
7. Surrender card to **manager or HR** on termination **or when requested**.
8. **Managers** must collect cards on termination / if Workday rescinds.
9. **Personal credit not tied**, yet holder **personally liable** for charges violating policy or lacking verification unless they prove legitimacy to Company satisfaction.
10. **BAML** issues/administers cards; Workday supplies BAML: **name**, **mailing address**, **DOB**, **phone**, **employee ID**.
11. After transfer, handling follows **BAML privacy** norms; permitted uses include administering the card/transactions/regulatory compliance — **no direct-marketing reuse** asserted in Agreement text.
12. BAML disclosure scope includes affiliates/agents/providers, card networks + fraud outfits, lawful regulators/court/law-enforcement disclosures, transferees of rights/obligations.
13. Raw links BAML privacy notice (“For further information…”).
14. **Fraud reports** inside **30 days**; Agreement states disputes may be barred afterward **without extenuating circumstances** and **lateness can make Workday liable** — coordinate Expense via **expenses@workday.com** if stuck.
15. **Lost/stolen/compromised:** call BAML **immediately** (**NA:** (866) 500-8262 · **EMEA/APAC:** (877) 436-0960) and submit a **Travel Card Request** inside Workday **within 24 hours**.

**Amazon Business (US geography only, Agreement add-on):**

- Okta tile for small miscellaneous buys **outside typical HQ contracts** → **Corporate Card**, **<$1,000**, business necessity; steer laptops/monitors/ergonomics through **Service Hub**; purchases already covered by coordinators/preferred vendors **stay off Amazon per clip.**

**Questions:** Agreement → **expenses@workday.com**.

## Claims

- **30-day** expense-report requirement and **30-day** fraud-notification window carry **financial/liability** implications (Agreement).
- Holder **liable** for inappropriate or unverified charges despite **no tie to personal credit score** (Agreement).
