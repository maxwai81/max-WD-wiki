# CHUBB Claim Kit — Multinational (Workday, Inc.)

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/CHUBB Claim Kit.pdf]]  
**Purpose:** **Medical emergency / evacuation / repatriation** routing vs **AD&D, inconvenience, reimbursement medical** claims by **country of permanent residence**.

## Summary

1. **Medical emergency, evacuation, repatriation of mortal remains** → **International SOS** (regional numbers below); SOS coordinates claim submission to insurer for payment.

2. **Other claims** → split by residence cohort:

### Region A — filed via North American claims channel

**Countries:** Australia, Canada, **Hong Kong**, India, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, Mexico, New Zealand, Singapore, South Africa, South Korea, Switzerland, Thailand, United States.

- **Email:** ChubbAandHClaims@Chubb.com  
- **Mail:** Chubb North American Claims, P.O. Box 5124, Scranton, PA 18505  
- **Fax:** 302-476-7857  
- **Customer service:** 1-800-336-0627 — hours **8:00–16:30 EST Mon–Fri**; after hours **302-476-6194**, **302-476-7201**  
- **Online:** https://www.chubbclaims.com/ace/us-en/welcome.aspx  

**Standard forms called out:** AD&D; Lost Baggage / Baggage Delay / Personal Property; Quarantine.

**Reporting checklist themes:** policyholder name, policy number, claim type, claimant contact → then bundle **form + evidence** (itinerary, police/medical/death docs for AD&D; proof of value for baggage; quarantine letter + positive test + lodging/meals + economy airfare elements per kit).
