# House Rent Reimbursement — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/House Rent Reimbursement]]  
**Source URL:** `https://benefits.workday.com/hk/house-rent-reimbursement`

## Summary

HK **Rental Reimbursement Programme** (tax-efficiency scheme). **First-time enrollees:** sign policy PDF (`Workday Rental Reimbursement Scheme 2023.pdf` in downloads), submit application to **workday.housing@hk.pwc.com** by **15 March** annually. **Existing participants:** annual **March** exercise with receipts and valid tenancy; **prepare monthly receipts** in advance. **Leavers:** submit docs **1 week before termination** — no acceptance after termination date.

## Claims

- Eligibility: **all permanent contract** Workmates, **voluntary**, **no waiting period** (join from start date).
- Workday reviews submissions for policy compliance; employee responsible for accuracy/authenticity.
