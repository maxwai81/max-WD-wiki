# Business Travel Accident & Business Travel Medical — Schedule of Benefits

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/Business Travel Accident & Business Travel Medical.pdf]]  
**Carrier:** Chubb Insurance — combined **Business Travel Accident** and **Business Travel Medical** schedule.  
**Workday policy #:** ADD N18767041  
**Schedule effective:** 2026-01-01 (per document).

## Summary

Single-page **Schedule of Benefits** (USD) for the Chubb programme referenced from the Workday HK benefits travel page. Use together with [[wiki/sources/Business Travel Medical and Security]] for **ISOS**, booking rules, high-risk routing, and claims logistics.

## Claims

- **Emergency out-of-country medical expenses:** benefit maximum **$500,000 USD**; **calendar-year deductible $0**; **coinsurance (Chubb)** **100%**; **prescription drugs** covered; **room & board** at average semi-private room rate. *(Supports [[wiki/sources/Business Travel Medical and Security]] marketing figure.)*
- **Medical evacuation / repatriation** and **repatriation of remains:** **100% of covered expenses** (per schedule wording).
- **Security evacuation:** **$100,000** USD maximum on this schedule.
- **Accidental death & dismemberment (AD&D):** “Covered per policy document” — dollar caps not on this schedule page.
- **Pre-existing conditions & pregnancy:** treated like other conditions; **emergency only** covered.
- **Emergency dental:** **injury only** and **alleviation of pain** covered — **no dollar maximum stated** on this schedule (contrast with narrative “unlimited” wording elsewhere; see [[wiki/sources/Business Travel Medical and Security]] **Open questions**).
- **Baggage / property (samples):** lost/damaged personal property **$2,500/trip**, **$500/item**; financial instruments **$2,500/trip**, **$500 cash**; baggage delay **$500/trip**, **8-hour** waiting period; lost baggage **$5,000/trip**, **$500/item**.
- **War risk:** marked covered on schedule; footnote points to **Benefits page** for **business travel to high-risk areas** and **standard coverage limits** in excluded locations.
- **Israel — business travel:** document states **AD&D**, **medical evacuation/repatriation**, **repatriation of remains**, and **emergency out-of-country medical** are each **limited to $100,000 USD**; **no coverage** for **security evacuation** and **travel inconvenience** benefits (terminology on schedule aligns with baggage/delay-type benefits).

## Exclusions (schedule-level)

- **Routine and/or preventative** medical services **not covered**.
