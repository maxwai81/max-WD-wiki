# Financial Wellbeing Support — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/Financial Wellbeing Support]]  
**Source URL:** `https://benefits.workday.com/hk/northstar`

## Summary

**Northstar**: **free, unlimited** access to **certified financial advisors** + tools for Workmates (country nuances apply). Enrollment at `northstarmoney.com/activate`. Login dashboard at `northstarmoney.com` with **Workday email** initially; **personal email optional** post-registration — **benefit enrollment is framed as confidential to Workday** in tone (“will not be shared” style).

## Claims

- **Taxable-benefit list** in clip includes **Hong Kong** among countries where taxation may apply — Workday messaging says Workday **can cover/withhold taxes** to minimize year-end impact and **protect net pay** (read raw carefully for HR implementation).
- **New joiners:** may enroll anytime after **day one** but access stated as **after first week**.
- Bereavement carve-out: **spouse/partner Northstar continuation 3 months** after Workmate death (FAQ section).
- Support contact: **support@northstarmoney.com**
