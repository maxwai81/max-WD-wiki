# Time Off in Lieu — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/Time Off in Lieu]]  
**Source URL:** `https://benefits.workday.com/hk/time-off-in-lieu`

## Summary

Compensatory **TOIL** when **business travel on weekends/public holidays** (normally discouraged). Requires **People Leader initiation/approval trail** documenting weekend/holiday travel; traveller should **not work** aside from travelling on those TOIL-qualified segments when policy says “only travel and not permitted to work” segment.

### Accrual

- **Same region weekend/holiday travel:** **0.5 day** TOIL  
- **Outside region OR ≥8 hours** weekend/holiday travel: **1 day** TOIL

### Consumption rules

- Book into Workday as **‘Time off in Lieu’** via **Request Absence** path in clip.
- **Use within six months** of return; **not payable on termination** → must consume.

### Volume guidance

- Policy anticipates seldom **>** ~**8 TOIL days** per calendar year; escalate with manager if workloads conflict.
