# Personal and Extended Leave of Absence — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/Personal and Extended Leave of Absence]]  
**Source URL:** `https://benefits.workday.com/hk/personal-and-extended-leave-of-absence`

## Summary

Structured **Personal Leave of Absence (PLOA)** programme separate from sickness/vacation.

### Standard PLOA

- Max **90 calendar days**.
- **Global** eligibility; **FTE, Regular, Interns**; **available from employment day 1** (clip).

### Extended PLOA

- From **91 days up to 12 months**.
- Requires **minimum 5 years’ service** (country exceptions: France **≥3 years** congé sabbatique framing per footnote); good performance standing; **no prior PLOA in previous two years**.
- Approval **case-by-case** to **SVP** (extended also **CHRO** per clip).

### Economics / protections

- **Unpaid**: salary and cash allowances cease; equity rules: **stock continues vesting 30 days** then **suspension** until return; LTD/pension nuances in letter; mostly benefits continue employee-funded portions per insurer T&Cs clip says.
- **PTO**: **EMEA** holiday accrual stops during LOA absent local mandate; Extended requires using **unused accrued PTO** before commencing per bullet.
- **Return:** employee must proactively contact manager ≥**1 month** before scheduled return.
