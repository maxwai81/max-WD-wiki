# Compassionate Leave — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/Compassionate Leave]]  
**Source URL:** `https://benefits.workday.com/hk/compassionate-leave`

## Summary

**Global compassionate leave:** up to **10 paid days per cause/event** for **standalone, unanticipated** life events **not** already covered by other programmes (e.g. sick leave). Broad enumerated event list (bereavement including pet, miscarriage/stillbirth, reproductive treatment, menopause, severe menstruation symptoms, divorce/separation, domestic abuse, home emergency, caregiving, travel for adoption/funeral/medical, donation, war/military-related emergencies, etc.).

## Claims

- If more than 10 days needed → discuss with **People Leader** and other leave policies.
- **US** employees directed to **Horizon** local page (clip) — HK page still describes global policy for non-US with US exception note.
- **Notify People Leader ASAP**; delayed notification allowed when circumstances require.
- **Good-faith expectation:** typically **≤1–2 uses per year** totalling **≤10 days** inclusive; misuse may trigger **reasonable evidence**; benefit may be withdrawn if systematically abused.
