# Sick leave enquiry — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/Sick leave enquiry]]

## Summary

Workflow question in the clip: whether a **doctor’s letter / sick certificate is required for a single calendar day** of **paid sick leave** in **Hong Kong** when applying on **WOW** (Workday off workflow).

**People & Purpose (HR) responses in thread (verbatim substance):**

- **Medical / sick certificate required** when sick leave is **for more than two days** (one message).
- Separately clarified: sick certificate **is not required** for **one day** sick leave.

**Meta from thread:** responder references Horizon (via Okta) for ongoing P&P, **People Guide request** reopen window (~7 days), and **Wex on Slack** for general P&P Q&A — operational routing only.

**Evidence type:** This is **one resolved HR interaction**, not necessarily the full statutory/policy document for HK sick-pay documentation. Prefer **published leave / sick policies** when they clash or when edge cases arise (exact meaning of **“more than two days,”** consecutive vs cumulative, partially paid sickness, etc.).

## Claims

- For **Hong Kong**, per this ticket, HR stated **no certificate** for **1 day** paid sick leave, and certification expected when the absence is **more than two days** — supported only by [[raw/Sick leave enquiry]].
