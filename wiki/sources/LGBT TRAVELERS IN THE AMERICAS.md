# LGBT travelers — regional safety guide (ISOS-style briefing)

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/LGBT TRAVELERS IN THE AMERICAS.pdf]]  
**Note:** Vault filename emphasizes **Americas**, but the PDF is a **multi-region** pack (**Americas**, **Asia/Pacific**, **Europe**, **MENA**, **Africa**) with shared “Worldwide Reach” branding — treat as **single duty-of-care briefing** for pre-trip awareness.

## Summary

Non-benefits **travel risk** content: legal status vs social hostility for **LGBT** travellers by region; repeatedly advises **low profile**, avoiding **public displays of affection**, **dress/accessories** that signal orientation, and **public debate** on LGBT rights where risky.

## Claims (actionable themes — not insurance terms)

- **Americas / Caribbean:** credible risk of **harassment** or **legal exposure** in some jurisdictions; **11 Caribbean** jurisdictions listed where **same-sex activity illegal** (Antigua & Barbuda, Barbados, Belize, Dominica, Grenada, Guyana, Jamaica, St Kitts & Nevis, St Lucia, St Vincent & Grenadines, Trinidad & Tobago). **Northern Triangle** called out for **violence** and **discrimination**. Country snapshots (Brazil leadership/language change 2019; Chile hostility uptick cited 2017; Argentina relatively permissive).
- **Asia-Pacific:** advises discretion even where prosecution unlikely; snapshot table (Malaysia, Singapore, NZ/Australia, Indonesia); notes **18** AP countries illegal + parts of Indonesia (**Sharia**); **death penalty** cited for Pakistan in snippet.
- **Europe:** higher assault risk statistically even in tolerant countries; discretion in Eastern Europe/Central Asia/rural areas; Hungary constitutional ban noted (2012); Malta/Belgium/UK/Denmark/Spain/Portugal/Norway/Finland cited as fewer difficulties; Russia/Central Asia nuances.
- **MENA:** **shath** framing; **GCC** illegality and severe penalties; Egypt **debauchery** statute; Iran enforcement; Israel caution in conservative areas; Jordan prosecution via other statutes; Morocco example sentencing — general guidance **low profile**, ignore provocations, leave area.
- **Africa:** legal variation; Cameroon, Gambia, DRC draft/adoption threads; **death penalty** cited Mauritania/parts Somalia/Nigeria under Sharia; general rural vs urban conservatism.
