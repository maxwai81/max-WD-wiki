## Summary

**Group medical** with **AIA** (broker **WISE** contacts in clip — Ray Lee etc.). Policy period **1 Feb–31 Jan**. **Permanent employees** from commencement, **≥15 hours/week**, **HK residence** requirement for employee + dependents. **Medical history disregard** enrolment window: **within 30 days** or underwriting applies.

## Claims (benefit design snippets)

- Outpatient/alternative caps examples: **HKD 1,000** GP/physio/chiro/osteopathy/TCM per visit/day; **HKD 2,000** non-panel specialist; **HKD 5,000/day** prescribed medication; **psych** max **20 visits/year**; **HKD 10,000 dental** allowance (figures per raw — verify SPD/certificate).

## Fertility add-on

- **HKD 50,000** per policy year **fertility treatment** limit enumerated on [[wiki/sources/AIA Health Insurance Coverage]] (Plan 001 modalities/remark **19**).
- Portal narrative previously described AIA + **Maven Wallet** (up to USD 25,000 additional) — **Maven Wallet is no longer available to HK employees effective 2026-02-01 (US only)**. AIA Plan 001 is now the **sole** insurance-backed fertility funding layer for HK. See [[wiki/concepts/Maven family and reproductive benefits]].
