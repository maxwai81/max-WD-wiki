# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy]]  
**Source URL:** `https://benefits.workday.com/hk/paid-time-off-policy`

## Summary

**Regional policy** (clipped from HK benefits URL) for **full-time, permanent** staff in **EMEA, APAC & Japan**: PTO must be recorded in Workday; company may intervene on balances and audits. Operates alongside **local** pages for entitlement amounts, carry-over deadlines, and encashment (links in raw to annual-leave and leave-entitlement paths on the same portal).

**Guardrails (as written):**

- **Take-up:** at least **20 calendar days** PTO per calendar year (1 Jan–31 Dec); at least **10 days** annual leave by **30 Sep** (“leave spreading”); quarterly **P&P** monitoring/reminders for low usage.
- **Notice:** **2 weeks** ahead for absences up to **3 working days**; **4 weeks** for longer; emergencies shorter notice with justification and line-manager approval; not approved until **confirmed in Workday**.
- **Peak / blackout:** possible **team caps** in key periods (examples: fiscal year-end, major launches, Workday Rising); managers announce blackouts and fair rotation.
- **Carry-over:** **5 days** standard cap; extension only for **statutory** or **approved business** cases; extended carry-over needs **documented PBP approval**; local law overrides where stricter/different.
- **Forfeiture:** unused accrued PTO may be **forfeited** (exception called out for **Australia and New Zealand** local law).
- **Notice period:** expect to **exhaust** remaining PTO during notice; People Leader has **discretion** on timing.
- **Ill on PTO:** contact People Leader immediately; with documentation per **local sick** policy, PTO can be **rebooked as sick**.
- **Back-to-back annual + sick:** may **trigger review**; repeated undocumented patterns → **formal P&P investigation**.
- **Discipline:** repeated/deliberate breach (including sick misuse, booking abuse, carry-over abuse) may lead to **disciplinary** action.

## Claims

- Workday may **require** scheduling leave to hit minimum targets or reduce excessive balances ([[raw/Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy]]).
- **Encashment** of PTO may be **delayed or withheld** pending audit if balance accuracy is questioned; detail deferred to local entitlement policy ([[raw/Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy]]).
