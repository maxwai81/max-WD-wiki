# Financial Investments — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/Financial Investments]]  
**Source URL:** `https://benefits.workday.com/hk/financial-investments`

## Summary

Hub comparing **ESPP**, **Mandatory Pension Plan (MPF)**, **Voluntary Pension**, and **Rental Reimbursement Program** for HK: contribution rates, tax treatment, timelines, access ages, what happens on termination, default providers (e.g. HSBC Hong Kong for pensions), **ESPP** discount and purchase cycle.

## Claims (from summary table in raw)

- ESPP: up to **15%** salary contribution, up to **15% discount**, **6-month** purchase cycle, earliest access **6 months**, **not** tax-free, voluntary.
- Mandatory pension: **5%** employee + **5%** company on **Relevant Income**, tax-free, until retirement, access **65**, mandatory.
- Voluntary pension: **5% company** of basic after mandatory employee portion, employee contribution N/A in table, tax-free, **65** access, voluntary.
- Rental reimbursement: **40%** of basic salary employee contribution, tax-free, **monthly** access, voluntary, **employee self-funded** in “default provider” row.
