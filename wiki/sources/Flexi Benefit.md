## Summary

HK **Flexi Benefit Programme** complements **corporate AIA medical**. Annual **reset 1 February** for 12 months; entitlement bands (employee / +partner / +children combinations) **HKD 10k / 12k / 15k** per [[wiki/sources/TribeGO App User Guide (Workday Hong Kong)]]. **Holiday subsidy** expenses **capped at 50%** of total annual flexi entitlement to encourage other categories (maternity, TCM, screening, etc.).

## Claims

- Claims via **TribeGo mobile app** only (register, QR or credentials).
- Submitted by **28th** of month → paid **next payroll**; **last submission end of February** or balance **forfeits** (no carry-forward).
- **Auto-enrolment**; flex amount from **first of month following join**, **prorated** for first year eligibility.
- **Life events:** entitlement changes when corporate medical tier changes (detail table HTML empty in clip — rely on TribeGo/policy).
- Eligibility: **full-time permanent** and **interns**; **new hire allocation pro-rated quarterly** ([[wiki/sources/TribeGO App User Guide (Workday Hong Kong)]]) vs portal clip wording — prefer PDF for disputes.
