# CHUBB Business Travel Program — overview brochure

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/CHUBB Business Travel Program.pdf]]  
**Audience:** Workday, Inc. eligible employees and **dependents** on **business travel**, including **personal deviations** while on a business trip (per opening paragraph).

## Summary

Marketing/overview PDF describing **Accidental Death & Dismemberment** (loss schedule as % of principal sum), **additional** bundled benefits (baggage delay, bereavement/trauma, carjacking, etc.), and **emergency response** benefits when outside **home country** or **country of permanent assignment**. Reinforces **International SOS** first contact for emergencies and gives **online claims** URL.

## Claims (high-signal)

- **AD&D:** loss amounts expressed as **100% / 50% / 25%** of **principal sum** for categories shown (life/multiple members/quadriplegia etc.); **multiple losses same accident → single largest benefit** paid.
- **Emergency response (outside home / assignment country):** emergency medical, evacuation, repatriation of remains, **quarantine**, **security evacuation** (outside home country only), **war risk** (outside home country, assignment country, **United States**, **Afghanistan**, **Iraq**, **Ukraine** — per brochure wording).
- **Member ID / assistance:** **11BCPH829218** — matches [[wiki/sources/Business Travel Medical and Security]] ISOS reference.
- **Claims:** **Benefits Administrator** for forms; online **https://www.chubbclaims.com/ace/us-en/welcome.aspx** (brochure URL).
- **Emergencies:** contact **International SOS** (see ID card); identify as Workday member with membership number; SOS coordinates authorization and insurer claim path.

## Exclusions (illustrative — not exhaustive)

Brochure lists AD&D-style exclusions: intentional self-harm/suicide (AD&D only), war/military duty, sickness/disease except limited bacterial carve-outs, aircraft crew/pilot felonies, etc. **Additional** exclusions apply to **security evacuation** — brochure directs to **Benefits Administrator**.

## Legal / brand notes

- Described as **brief description**, **not an insurance contract**. Assistance: **International SOS**; security assistance/consultation: joint venture **International SOS** and **Control Risks**. Benefits **underwritten by ACE American Insurance Company**. **Chubb NA** referenced as US operating division.

## Contradictions

- **War risk geography** wording here vs [[wiki/sources/Business Travel Accident & Business Travel Medical]] footnote on high-risk areas — reconcile case-by-case; **schedule + portal** take precedence for Workday HK programme specifics.
