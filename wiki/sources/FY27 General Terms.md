# FY27 General Terms

**Raw:** [[raw/FY27 General Terms.pdf]] — Workday FY2027 **Sales Compensation Plan** administrative terms (marked confidential in footer). Approved signature block in source: **Robert Enslin**, President, Chief Commercial Officer, **2/1/2026**.

## What it is

The **General Terms** plus each participant’s **Individual Goal Sheet** define the FY2027 Workday Sales Compensation Plan. Together they set eligibility, metrics, quota/credit rules, commission math and payment mechanics, governance, and incorporation of **Sales guidelines** (including material in **Seismic**) and **country-specific addendum(s)** where applicable.

## Plan shell

- **Plan Period:** **1 Feb 2026** through **31 Jan 2027** (no work later than that end date is stated as in-scope).
- **Documents:** (1) these General Terms; (2) participant-specific **Individual Goal Sheet** (quota, weightings, rates, bonuses if any).
- **Earn / pay gate:** No incentive is earned until **both** General Terms and Individual Goal Sheet are **signed, acknowledged, and accepted**; until then, document says only **base salary** is in scope for the Plan Period.

## Structure (major sections)

High-level table of contents in source includes: purpose; eligibility; plan period; job responsibilities; base salary vs incentive compensation; performance period; territory; quota (linearity, proration, quarterly true-ups for certain roles, FX); new hires/transfers (ramp, **recoverable draw**); **qualified vs earned attainment**; commission calculation, payment dates, **windfall** policy, overpayment recovery, **opportunity holdover**, **open territory**, **splits**; plan transfers / close-out / **true-up**; **leaves of absence**; **termination**; administration and management discretion; deal-structure policies (minimum deal threshold, legal/finance approval paths, discount approval, documentation); **Code of Conduct**; plan confidentiality; U.S. **at-will** / discretionary benefit framing; governance (acceptance, supersession, interpretation, severability, **side letter** and **settlement** prohibitions); definitions appendix; acknowledgment.

## Leaves of absence (sales plan lens)

- **Qualified Leaves** (examples): maternity, bonding, medical leave, sabbatical — as defined by **People & Purpose (P&P)**. **Non-Qualified** examples include personal and educational leaves. Classification via **People Guide Request**.
- **Commission during leave:** During a **Qualified Leave**, may earn or be **advanced** commission up to **26 weeks per event**. **Non-Qualified:** **not** eligible to earn or be advanced commission during absence.
- **Individual Quota participants:** Qualified Leave **≥ six consecutive weeks** → eligible for payments equal to **75%** of **monthly Target Incentive Compensation** for up to **26 weeks**.
- **Team / Territory Quota participants (incl. Sales Managers):** On Qualified Leave, may remain eligible to earn commissions/bonuses under current plan up to **26 weeks**.
- **Coverage:** If manager cannot cover deals during an Individual Quota participant’s LOA, another Individual Quota participant with similar product eligibility may cover via **Exception Request** (approved before work). Covering participant: **100%** commission + quota credit on closed **Net New / Add-On**; **renewals**: commissions only.
- More detail referenced in **Sales Comp Corner** (see Definitions in source).

## Termination / ineligible transfer

On termination or transfer to an **ineligible** role (including non-working status), only compensation **earned under the Plan as of Last Day of Work** or transfer effective date applies unless law requires otherwise; **no** commission for opportunities closing **after** those dates.
