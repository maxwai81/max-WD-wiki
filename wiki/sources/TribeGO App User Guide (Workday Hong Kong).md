# TribeGO App User Guide — Workday Hong Kong (Flex 2026–2027)

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/TribeGO_App_User_Guide_Workday_Hong_Kong_2026_copy.pdf.pdf]]  
**Cover:** Workday Flexible Benefits **1 Feb 2026 – 31 Jan 2027**.

## Summary

Combined PDF: (1) **Flex wallet** amounts, eligibility, deadlines, **claim category** matrix and detailed **claim rules**; (2) **TribeGO** registration, password reset, flex claim submission, records, documents, marketplace. Supplements portal clip [[wiki/sources/Flexi Benefit]] with **tabular rules** the HTML clip omitted.

## Claims — wallet tiers (HKD)

| Tier | Annual allocation |
|------|-------------------|
| Employee | 10,000 |
| Employee + Spouse | 12,000 |
| Employee + Child | 15,000 |
| Employee + Family (Spouse + Child) | 15,000 |

- **Eligibility:** Full-time **permanent** employees and **interns**.
- **Pro-ration:** allocation **pro-rated quarterly** for **new hires**.
- **Leavers:** allocation **not** pro-rated on exit — submit claims **before termination date**, preferably **last day of month** for last payroll reimbursement.
- **Monthly cut-off:** **28th** each month. **Year-end claims cut-off:** **28 Feb 2027**. **Unutilised balance forfeited**.
- **Receipt rule:** dated within **benefit year**. **Overseas transactions allowed.** **Voucher-paid** purchases (cash/mall/government vouchers) **not eligible**.
- **Payroll timing:** submitted **and approved** by **last day of month** → paid **following month’s payroll**.

## Claimable categories (grid headline)

Categories include: excess medical (GP/specialist not covered by AIA); dental; maternity; TCM; optical; vaccination; health/preventative screening; **holiday subsidy (50% cap)**; cosmetic dentistry; chiropractic/homeopathy; **mental wellness — doctor/medical** and **separate psychologist (non-medical)** rows; health supplements; fitness. **Dependent tax eligibility** varies by category (spouse+child columns **Y/N** per grid — see raw for full matrix).

## Claim rules (high-signal excerpts)

- **Excess medical:** claimable includes GP/specialist illness/injury consultation/medication; **not claimable** includes chronic cash-pay carve-outs, MRI/CT, specialist **without referral**, paediatrician pathways, medical report fees, balance beyond insurer limits, **physiotherapy**, compulsory infant vaccinations list, retail pharmacy “medicinal drugs” framing vs exclusions — plus cosmetic treatments (**Botox**/fillers etc.).
- **Dental:** basic care claimable; **not** dentures, implants, crowns, bridges, orthodontics, appliances/splint, braces, hygiene counselling, retail oral hygiene products.
- **Maternity:** scans, outpatient check-ups, packages, delivery/stay claimable; **not** fertility/IVF, post-natal massage, confinement, non-medical termination.
- **Holiday subsidy:** **50% of allocation cap**; claim **after** travel; boarding pass + accommodation; travel date inside benefit period; detailed eligible transport/lodging vs excluded meals/petrol/fines/concerts/visa etc.
- **Fitness:** **monthly fees** only (annual memberships **prorate** by month); gyms/programmes from **registered businesses**; excludes ClassPass-style generic memberships, country clubs, golf, freelance PT, deposits, apparel.

## TribeGO logistics

- Download **TribeGO** from app stores; register via **welcome email** link or **QR** (QR described as stable for resets).
- Support: **hello@ltria.com**, **Mon–Fri** (excl. public holidays) **9am–6pm**.
- In-app: **Flex Benefit** → submit claims (camera/paperclip upload), **Claim Records**, **Flex Wallet** category explorer; **Others → Documents**; **Member Exclusives** → marketplace shop / vouchers.
