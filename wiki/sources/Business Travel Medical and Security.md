## Summary

**Duty of care** stack for business travel: **International SOS (ISOS)** for 24/7 medical/security coordination; **Chubb** **Business Travel Accident (BTA)** / **Business Travel Medical** (out-of-home-country urgent/emergent care). **Design intent:** primary medical/security assistance for travel **outside home/payroll country**; in home country, **use local medical insurance** for illness/injury while travelling for business (per table). **Domestic** travellers: BTA covers AD&D, evacuation/repatriation if **>100 miles** from normal residence, baggage/personal property limits as stated.

## Claims (high-signal)

- Out-of-country emergency medical (Chubb): portal prose cites **$500,000 USD**; **Chubb Schedule of Benefits** ([[wiki/sources/Business Travel Accident & Business Travel Medical]]) confirms **$500,000** emergency out-of-country medical, **$0** deductible, **100%** coinsurance, plus evacuation/repatriation **100% of covered expenses**, **security evacuation $100,000**, baggage/property sub-limits — **prefer PDF schedule** over web bullets when they diverge.
- **Emergency dental:** portal paragraph reads like **unlimited**; PDF schedule states **injury only** and **alleviation of pain** covered **without a stated dollar cap** on that page — align wording to schedule scope; full policy may still define limits.
- **Pre-travel:** download **ISOS app** with Workday email; book via **Egencia**; elevated-risk destinations trigger **ISOS pre-travel notice**.
- **High-risk areas** (listed: Afghanistan, Iraq, Russia, Ukraine, Israel, Gaza, West Bank, Lebanon): **contact Global Workplace Safety before booking**; possible supplemental AD&D; after approval, raise **People Guide Request** for Benefits-arranged travel insurance; mandatory briefing and VP+ approval patterns; non-compliance can cancel trip / discipline / termination (per clip). **Israel carve-out on Chubb schedule:** several medical/evacuation maximums drop to **$100,000 USD each**, and **security evacuation** plus **travel inconvenience** benefits are **excluded** — see [[wiki/sources/Business Travel Accident & Business Travel Medical]].
- **Personal travel** with business: **not covered** as standalone personal trip; **in conjunction** with business up to **14 days** before/during/end of business trip — **ISOS advice** available; personal independent travel not covered by travel insurance per FAQ tone.
- **Claims:** coordinate via **ISOS** for direct pay; self-pay then **Chubb** (jurisdiction-specific portals; policy numbers **ADD N18767041** vs **UKBMND41936** by country list in clip). **Cigna MBA:** expenses **before 2026-01-01** may claim via Cigna Envoy within 365 days; **after 2026-01-01** via **Chubb** (explicit transition note in raw).
- **Travel delay / inconvenience:** **not covered** by Workday travel insurance (FAQ).
- **ISOS membership portal** number in clip: **11BCPH829218** (for pre-trip health intel).
