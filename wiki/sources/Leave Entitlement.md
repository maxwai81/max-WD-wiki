# Leave Entitlement — source digest

**Canonical raw:** [[raw/Leave Entitlement]]  
**Source URL:** `https://benefits.workday.com/hk/leave-entitlement`

## Summary

**Master table** of HK leave types as presented on benefits site: **Annual/PTO**, **Public Holidays**, **Floating (2 days/yr)**, **Unpaid**, **TOIL**, **Personal & Extended LOA**, **Sabbatical (30 biz days paid)**, **Sick**, **Maternity** (40 weeks text in table), **Enhanced Bonding**, **Caregiving (12 wks / rolling 12 mo, 50% pay)**, **Global Compassionate (10 days/event)**, **Bright Days (3/yr)**.

## Contradictions (**important**)

- Table row **“Personal and Extended Leave of Absence | From 1 month to 1 year, available after 5 years…”** **oversimplifies / conflicts** with detailed policy on [[raw/Personal and Extended Leave of Absence]]: **standard PLOA up to 90 calendar days** exists **from day one** for eligible populations, while **extended** segment (91 days–12 months) **requires 5 years’ service** plus other conditions.

**Resolution for wiki users:** treat [[wiki/sources/Personal and Extended Leave of Absence]] as authoritative for PLOA mechanics; use this table as orientation only.
