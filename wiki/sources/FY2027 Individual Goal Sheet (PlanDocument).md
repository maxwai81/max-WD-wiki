# FY2027 Individual Goal Sheet (PlanDocument)

**Raw:** [[raw/PlanDocument.pdf]] — exported **FY2027 Individual Goal Sheet** instance (Workday workflow: manager approval + participant acceptance timestamps in source).

**Privacy:** Base salary, quotas, commission rates, and other participant-specific figures are **only** in the raw PDF. This digest records **structure and mechanics**, not a copy of compensation tables.

## Role of the Goal Sheet

Per [[wiki/sources/FY27 General Terms]], the Individual Goal Sheet is the participant-specific half of the Plan: territories, **ACV** quota schedule, **Target Incentive Compensation** weighting, attainment tiers and **commission rates**, and acknowledgements. **Metric Definition and Product Eligibility** (referenced in the PDF) govern eligible solutions and sales motions.

## Structural facts (from this instance)

- **Role family:** Sales Role — **Solution Consultant** (title as shown in raw).
- **Plan effective date:** **02/01/2026**; **performance period:** **Annual**.
- **Territory split (Target Incentive allocation):** **Territory 1** — **60%** on **Territory New & Renewal ACV**; **Territory 2** — **40%** on **Territory New + Renewal ACV** (total 100%). Quota credit follows qualifying Territory New & Renewal ACV per metric rules.
- **Territory labels in source:** Territory 1 **HK / TW**; Territory 2 **APAC**. Manager named in PDF export.
- **Quarterly quota rows** present for **QTR-1-2027** through **QTR-4-2027** and **YEAR-2027** for each territory block (numeric detail in raw only).

## Commission crediting and payment (as stated on sheet)

- **Smaller deals:** Opportunities with commissions **< USD 250,000** — **100%** of eligible sales commissions credited after **Closed/Won** and close date passed.
- **Larger / supersede:** **Supersede & Replace** opportunities, or opportunities with **anticipated commissions > USD 250,000** — **50%** credited after Closed/Won and close date passed; additional 50% rule references **review** + **two months in arrears** after closed/won (see raw wording).
- **Payment timing:** Generally **one month in arrears** of the crediting event; **February** exception: payments **three months in arrears** (May).
- **Tiers:** Attainment tiers (e.g. **0–100%** vs **100%+** against **Assigned ACV Quota**) map to different **commission rates** per territory block — exact rates in raw.

## Acknowledgements and acceptance

Document repeats that Goal Sheet + **FY2027 General Terms** define the Plan; company may modify/terminate Plan without retroactive reduction of already-earned commissions; **at-will** framing where allowed; authorization to **reconcile earned commissions against unearned Recoverable Draw** across fiscal plans subject to law. **Electronic accept:** user must **Accept** **both** General Terms **and** Individual Goal Sheet to be eligible for commission payouts — per boilerplate in source.

Workflow status in export: manager **APPROVED** and participant **APPROVED** with timestamps (PDT) in raw.
