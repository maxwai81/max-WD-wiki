We actively encourage you to take time off. That’s why we offer a range of paid leave options to cover all eventualities, both planned and unplanned. You can book vacation time, enjoy family time — especially when welcoming a new family member — volunteer your time or take a timeout when you’re not feeling your best. Even better, requesting time off is easy. Simply submit your request via Workday or talk to your manager.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Dreaming of the beach, exploring the Seven Wonders or taking that cooking course?

No problem. Each calendar year, we offer full-time Workmates paid annual leave. If you work part time, or you’re on a fixed-term contract, you also get paid leave but it will be prorated. What’s important is that you take essential time to rest and recharge, however you see fit.

## Public Holidays

While Public Holidays vary from country to country, Workday offices will close on the respective dates and you will receive full pay for Taking Time Off.

We actively encourage you to check the calendar of Public Holidays relevant to your country, via this portal or on Workday, so you can enjoy bonus days off with friends, family and pets.

## Parental Leave

Time off for family and dependants is important, which is why we offer a range of options to cover all stages of parenthood, from welcoming a newborn into the family fold to adopting a young child.

While the statutory allowance varies from country to country, Workday’s priority is that you take time to bond with your newest family member without worrying about your pay, so offer Workmates 12-weeks paid Bonding Leave. Parental Leave also extends beyond the early years of childcare, so you can be there for your loved ones, no matter what.

## Sick Leave

Taking care of your health is one of our top priorities and we know that time away from work is sometimes the only way to get well.

In order to give you peace of mind and help your recovery, Workday allows you to claim Paid Sick Leave up to a limit each calendar year. You also have options for looking after poorly dependents, too. From time to time, you or your dependents may need longer to get back to health, which is why Workday has leave of absence policies in place to help you take whatever time you need.

## Other Leave

Whatever life holds for you, we recognise the many different reasons you may need to Take Time Off.

You may have jury service, require flexibility around caregiving responsibilities such as looking after an elderly relative or maybe you need time to grieve for a much-loved pet. You can also request compassionate leave if a loved one is seriously ill. That said, extended time off doesn’t always need to be serious. If you’ve been with us for more than five years, you can take up to one year off unpaid to do whatever you please, and Workday will continue to pay for some benefits during that time, too.
