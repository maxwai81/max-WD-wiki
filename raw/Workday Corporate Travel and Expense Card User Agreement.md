This Workday Corporate Travel and Expense Card User Agreement (“T&E Card Agreement”) describes agreements, procedures and obligations that apply to use of Workday Corporate Travel and Expense Cards (T&E Cards). After you have reviewed and acknowledged this T&E Card agreement, you will be issued a Workday T&E Card. This will save you from having to pay for travel and other out-of-pocket business expenses and then request reimbursement.

1. This Agreement should be read in accordance with the Travel and Expense Policy, which sets forth the expectations related to business expenses.
2. T&E cards are to be used for business purposes only.
3. T&E Cards are to be used for ***all*** business-related travel expenses.
4. T&E Cards are to be used only by the workmate whose name appears on the card (the “T&E Cardholder”).
5. T&E Cardholders ***must*** submit expense reports and required documentation for all charges made to the card for approval within 30 days of the transaction date.
6. Failure to follow the policies set forth in this T&E Card agreement and the Travel and Expense Policy may result in your card being suspended or revoked, as well as disciplinary action, up to and including termination of your employment.
7. T&E Cardholders must surrender their card to their manager or HR upon termination or upon manager or HR’s request.
8. Managers are responsible for collecting T&E Cards upon termination of the Cardholder’s employment or if Workday decides to rescind or collect the card for any reason.
9. Your personal credit will not be tied to this account. However, you will be personally responsible for any charged amounts that are in violation of any Company policy, or that are not appropriately verified, unless you can otherwise show to the Company’s satisfaction that the charge is an appropriate expense.
10. T&E Cards will be issued and administered by Bank of America (“BAML”). In order to issue a new T&E Card to you, Workday will provide the following required information about you to BAML: Your name, Your mailing address, Your date of birth; Your phone number; Your employee ID number
11. Once this information is provided to BAML, BAML’s use of the information will be governed by its own privacy policy. BAML may use your information for any permitted purpose, such as to administer your card, facilitate transactions, and comply with applicable regulations. Bank of America will not use your data for direct marketing.
12. BAML may disclose your information (as well as any other information you directly provide to BAML) to: a.their affiliates, agents, auditors and service providers; b.card networks, and fraud prevention agencies; c.to any other person if legally required, including to law enforcement agencies, authorities, regulators and courts; d.any other person to whom we may transfer or intend to transfer, assign or sell any of our rights or obligations under the policy.
13. [For further information about how BAML may use your data, click here](https://www.bankofamerica.com/security-center/online-privacy-notice/ "For further information about how BAML may use your data, click here")
14. Report any fraudulent activity with BAML within 30 days. Any potential fraudulent activity is unable to be disputed following the 30 day time period absent extenuating circumstances, and if not reported within that time Workday becomes liable for the expense. If added support is needed by the Workday Expense team please email expenses@workday.com
15. If your card is lost, stolen, or compromised you are required to:

a. Notify the bank ***immediately*** by calling Bank of America;

- North America: (866) 500-8262
- EMEA/APAC: (877) 436-0960

b. Submit a Travel Card Request in Workday within 24 hours to notify the card program administrator.

**Amazon Business:**

If you are located in the U.S., an Okta tile is provided to all cardholders to order small, miscellaneous

items that aren’t covered typically by Workday contracts. Items purchased on your card must be less

than $1000 and for business purpose only. You must use your Corporate Card and not a personal card.

You should continue to obtain items like laptops, monitors, and ergonomic stands through Service Hub as

you do today. Items purchased through another preferred supplier (e.g., office supplies via Office

Coordinators) should not be purchased using Amazon Business.

If you have any questions about this T&E Card agreement, please email expenses@workday.com.
