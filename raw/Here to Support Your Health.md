## Family Support at your Fingertips

Virtual care with Maven, designed for you and your family.

## No matter where you are on your reproductive and family health journey, Maven is here

Maven is our partner in holistic family, reproductive and menopause benefits, always available at no cost to \*Workmates. You can begin using Maven at any stage in your fertility or parenting journey, now with continuous support through your child’s tenth birthday.

Get free 24/7 virtual access to top-rated providers via unlimited video appointments, messaging, and classes—all from the comfort of your home.

*\*Workday employees, their partners and dependent children (up to age 26) receive unlimited resources, appointments and messaging through Maven at no cost.*

## Workday’s comprehensive Maven offering includes:

- Maven - All Workmates have access to Maven’s on-demand suite of virtual support, content, and community for every stage of family planning.
- Maven Wallet - Reimbursement for adoption, fertility, and surrogacy expenses, up to local lifetime mximum per Workmate.
- Maven Menopause - A specialised support program for every stage of the menopause journey.
- New in 2023 - Maven Milk - To help ease the transition back to work travel for breastfeeding parents, Maven provides the gear to help you safely store and travel with milk. Plus, [lactation consultants](https://benefits.workday.com/file/218) are now available to help you reach your breastfeeding goals.
- New in 2023 - Maven Parenting & Paediatrics - An expansion of the original Maven virtual programme, Workparents now have access to Maven paediatricians and children’s behavioural care support through a child’s tenth birthday
