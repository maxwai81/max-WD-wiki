## New benefit

Fertility benefits

We're excited to announce improved fertility treatment coverage to support you in growing your family.

What's covered:

You now have access to a comprehensive range of fertility treatments, including:

- Consultations, investigations, and medications
- IVF, ICSI, GIFT, and ZIFT procedures
- Artificial insemination (including IUI)
- Cryopreservation and embryo storage
- Egg freezing for delayed pregnancy
- Embryo transport

You have a total of **HKD 50,000** for treatment, which includes:

1. **AIA benefit:** You should use your AIA benefit first, which covers up to HKD 50,000
2. **Maven wallet:** If you need more than HKD 50,000 for treatment, you can use your [Maven Wallet](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/file/1d5d881c92891eb417fe69d46884fe53c9a75682ebf56f60a834c990f7daf32f?i=1) to cover the remaining costs, up to a maximum of $25,000
3. **This benefit cannot exceed a total of $25,000**

This benefit covers both inpatient and outpatient services. For more detail on the coverage, please reach out to the broker. Contact information can be found on this page in the 'provider details' box.

**Important notes:**

- Treatments not listed above are not covered
- Reversal of sterilisation and treatment of miscarriage related to failed fertility treatment are not covered
- Coverage ends once a pregnancy is confirmed

We understand the challenges faced when starting a family and hope this enhanced benefit provides valuable support during this important time.

## Who is eligible?

All permanent employees on commencement of employment and working a minimum of 15 hours per week.

Casuals and Contractors are not eligible for cover.

Only employees and the dependents residing in Hong Kong can be covered.
