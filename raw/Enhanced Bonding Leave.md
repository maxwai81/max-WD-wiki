We’ve rolled out a new globally consistent benefit for new parents: you will now be eligible to take up to 12 weeks of paid leave and receive 100% of your base salary to welcome and bond with a new addition to your family (birth or adoption of a child). In line with our core values and commitment to VIBE (Value Inclusion, Belonging, and Equity), this benefit is available to all new parents, according to each country's specific local laws, regulations and healthcare policies.

## Key Takeaways

- The Parental Leave is available to Workmates from their first day as an employee
- The child must have been born or adopted after the Workmates hire date in order to be eligible for the Enhanced Bonding Leave
- In countries where the current benefit exceeds 12 weeks of base pay, you’ll continue to receive the greater benefit, and there will be no change
- Enhanced Bonding Leave and pay is inclusive of any legal entitlement you have in your country
- Please note public holidays do not accrue under this leave type for UK and Ireland

## Workbabies

From the entire Workday family, congratulations on your newest addition! Workbaby kits will be sent automatically to Workmates who complete the parental leave process directly from the vendor, within 8-12 weeks of submitting the leave request.

If you have additional questions, would like to opt out or have special circumstances regarding this gift, please open a [People Guide Request](https://wd5.myworkday.com/workday/email-universal/task/2997$18295.htmld) to connect with someone directly.

\*Workday will report the gift value as income, where required by law, and make a reasonable effort to pay the required tax withholdings on your behalf. Workday will remit an estimated tax amount on an upcoming paystub.
