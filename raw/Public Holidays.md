## What’s in it for me?

Our offices are closed on the following company-paid public holidays and you do not need to book these days, as they should be pre-loaded in Workday:

**2026**

- First Day of January - Thursday, 1 January 2026
- Lunar New Year's Day - Tuesday, 17 February 2026
- The Second Day of Lunar New Year - Wednesday, 18 February 2026
- The Third Day of Lunar New Year - Thursday, 19 February 2026
- Thank You Day - Friday, 27 March 2026
- Good Friday - Friday, 3 April 2026
- Easter Monday & Ching Ming Festival (Observed) -Monday, 6 April 2026
- Easter Monday (Observed) - Tuesday, 7 April 2026
- Labor Day - Friday, 1 May 2026
- Thank You Day - Friday, 22 May 2026
- Buddha's Birthday (Observed) - Monday, 25 May 2026
- Thank You Day - Thursday, 18 June 2026
- Tuen Ng (Dragon Boat) Festival (observed) - Monday, 22 June 2026
- Juneteenth - Friday, 19 June 2026
- Hong Kong Special Administrative Region Establishment Day - Wednesday, 1 July 2026
- Thank You Day - Friday, 4 September 2026
- Day After Chinese Mid-Autumn Festival - Saturday, 26 September 2026
- National Day - Thursday, 1 October 2026
- Chung Yeung Festival (Observed) - Monday, 19 October 2026
- Chinese Winter Solstice Festival or Christmas Day - Friday, 25 December 2026
- First weekday after Christmas Day - Saturday, 26 December 2026

\*Please note public holidays may be subject to change, dependent on an official announcement from the state. Please refer back to this page for any updates.
