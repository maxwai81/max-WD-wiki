## Supporting your Child’s Mental Health

It is hard enough managing our own mental health in addition to being responsible for another person’s mental well-being. Help is at hand with a range of mental health resources to support parents and children alike.

We hear a lot in the news about behavioural health, and the challenges facing our kids.  
  
If your child is struggling with their mental health and they need some help, you may be feeling really worried as a parent – and also like you’re not sure where to start. Remember that you and your child are not alone.

## Supporting you and your family

All Workmates have access to support services. Both [Optum](https://benefits.workday.com/eap) and [Maven](https://benefits.workday.com/family-support-with-maven) provide clinical care to youth and families.  
  
From onsite Workplace Mental Health Consultants, mental health services to parenting and pediatric programmes, providing access to:

- Developmental and child psychologists
- Pediatric and adult mental health providers
- Family therapists
- Pediatricians
- Pediatric sleep coaches
- and more
