Enrich your financial wellness. Get practical and personalised support across our benefits and compensation packages, as well as help to identify your financial goals and your path to achieve them.

## What’s in it for me?

[Northstar](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ooJ588zJCHbI46s8YC_FMMUWwT5o3D7H/view?usp=sharing) provides you with unlimited access to Certified Financial Advisors and personal financial tools to help you make the most of your money at no cost.

[Sign up](http://www.northstarmoney.com/activate) and get matched with a personal Northstar financial advisor who will work with you one-on-one to understand your financial and life goals and build a custom plan to help get you there.

In addition to helping with budgeting, saving, debt management, and investing, your Northstar advisor is already familiar with Workday’s total compensation and benefits package and can help you make the most of everything we have to offer, including how you can use your salary, benefits, health care, equity, and perks based on your finances and priorities. Learn more about [Northstar](https://benefits.workday.com/file/270).

## How can I access Northstar?

Head to [www.northstarmoney.com/activate](http://www.northstarmoney.com/activate) to get started. Once you sign up, you will be matched with a dedicated Northstar certified financial planner who will reach out to you to get started. From there, you have unlimited access to your advisor and can connect with them through Northstar’s website chat feature, mobile app, and Zoom and phone calls. Remember to bookmark the link for quick access.

To access your Northstar dashboard once you’ve registered:

- Head to [https://www.northstarmoney.com/](https://www.northstarmoney.com/)
- Login with your **Workday** **email address**
- You can then change your preference to match your **personal email address,** once your registration is complete

**New Joiners** can enrol anytime after your first day and you'll will be able to access the services after your first week at Workday.

**How does Northstar ensure my data is safe?  
  
**Northstar take security very seriously. They follow data protection and privacy law for securing our users’ information and passwords. All financial information is encrypted at rest and in transit. There are strict permissions from the Northstar side, so only those who are able to assist you will have access to your financial information (i.e. your Financial Advisor).
