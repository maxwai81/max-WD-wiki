## What’s in it for me?

To support your financial wellbeing, Workmates based in Hong Kong are eligible for the Hong Kong Rental Reimbursement Program - allowing you to achieve maximum tax efficiency.

## How does it work?

**If you are enrolling for the first time**

You'll need to read and sign the Hong Kong Rental Reimbursement Policy in the downloads. Then simply submit the application form to [workday.housing@hk.pwc.com](mailto:workday.housing@hk.pwc.com) no later than the **15th of March each year**.

**For existing employees**

Please note that it is annual exercise whereby the collection of application form and receipts are in March of every year. However you are encouraged to prepare monthly receipt in advance and ensuring the tenancy agreement is valid for the current year end filing. A new form is needed to be submitted along with the rental receipt and tenancy agreement (for renewal / change of tenancy agreement).

You are responsible for the accuracy and authenticity of the submitted claim. Workday will review the claims submitted by employees to ensure that the Rental Reimbursement Policy has been complied with.

We encourage you to review and check the submission process and deadline on an annual basis to avoid any disruption of the year end filing timeline.

**If you're leaving Workday**

Please provide and submit the relevant documents 1 week before the termination date. Workday will not accept the submission after the termination date.

## Who is eligible?

All Workmates on a permanent contract are eligible to enrol into this program on a voluntary basis. There is no waiting period applied, this means all new joiners can enrol into the program from the start date.
