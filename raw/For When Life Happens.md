We understand that life can take unexpected turns. Be it a change in your financial or family situation, illness or the need for time off, we’ve got your back. Workday is here to support you through times of difficulty with a variety of benefits and resources, both online and onsite, to help you through all kinds of challenging events and circumstances.

## Death of a loved one

Losing a loved one is something no one should go through alone. Whether it’s a family member, a friend or a pet, we’re here to support you in every way that we can.

We want you to take the time you need, when you need it. Everyone handles grief differently and we understand you may need more support to come to terms with your loss. In addition to compassionate leave, you can access professional counselling services.

## Divorce or separation

Whether expected or unexpected, the breakdown of a marriage or a significant long-term relationship can impact every aspect of your life from work to home, money and family.

We understand that you may need to take some time away from work to sort your plans, deal with legalities or simply come to terms with the emotional impact of such a significant life event. You can also seek counselling support and financial advice to help you rebuild your life.

## Moving house

Moving house can throw all kinds of curveballs, whether you’re renting or buying. We appreciate the challenging nature of understanding the finances involved as well as moving everything you own from one place to another.

Talking to a financial advisor can put you straight on the numbers and utilising your time off options removes the worry of being at work when you need to get settled or decorate. Moving into a new home is ultimately a positive life event and we want to make sure your move goes as smoothly as possible.

## Salary continuation when you’re sick

Your health and wellbeing is our priority. We understand the importance of removing financial worries during times of illness so you can focus on your health.

That’s why you’ll still receive a portion of your salary when you need to take an extended leave of absence – check your local leave entitlement for further details. Whether you’re managing a long-term health condition or recovering from injury or surgery, if you need time off work to get well, we’re here for you.

## Looking after a loved one with ill health

Sometimes it’s not you who gets sick, but those closest to you.

It could be an immediate family member or a loved one who’s elderly, vulnerable or has special needs, and we want to lend a helping hand while you care for them. If taking time off work to act as caregiver is essential to their getting and staying well, you can request compassionate leave.

## A change in your financial situation

Trying to make financial plans can be difficult as it’s hard to know what may be around the corner.

Getting professional opinions and recommendations on your financial world, and how it may be changing, can provide you with a sense of direction and peace of mind. Northstar’s team of financial advisers allow Workmates to navigate through life events with a secure plan, so no matter whether your change is good, bad, permanent or temporary, you know how to stay in control.
