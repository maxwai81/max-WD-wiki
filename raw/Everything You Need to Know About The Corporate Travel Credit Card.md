**Corporate Travel & Expense Credit Card**

Need to request a new corporate card, change your limit, cancel your corporate card, or report your current corporate card as lost or stolen? [Please submit your request here!](https://wd5.myworkday.com/workday/d/task/2997$12683.htmld "Please submit your request here!")

**Activate your new Travel & Expense Corporate Card**

**1.Call the number on the label affixed to your card**

Information requested will vary by region. You may be asked to provide:

- Your credit limit; it is shown on the mailer to which your card is affixed
- Your date of birth
- Your phone number as shown on your Workday profile
- Your billing address; it is the address shown on the mailer to which your card was attached.

**2.US, Canada, and all APAC Workmates will also need:**

**Verification ID or Activation ID: it is “9” followed by your Workday ID**

- Example: If your Workday ID is 392, then your verification / activation ID would be 9392. *Don't remember your Workday employee ID? Check your profile in Workday – Employee ID is at the top of the page.*

**3\. If you cannot be automatically verified, you will be passed through to a BAML Associate and asked to provide:**

- Password (Workday ID)
- Home Address

**How to view your PIN and setup notifications**

**1\. Obtain your PIN online**

1. **Go to www.bofaml.com/globalcardaccess**
2. **Select 'Register a card' on the right side of the screen.**
3. **Information requested will vary by region. You may be asked to provide:**
- 16 digit card number
- name as it appears on the card (in all CAPS)
- credit limit (no commas, decimal points, or currency symbols)
- security code (3 digits on back of card)
- date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY)
- Your PIN will be displayed after the one-time registration.

Please note that after setting your PIN, it may take 2-3 attempts for the PIN to authenticate and set. If it fails, enter the PIN again until it is successful.

**2\. Card Notifications**

- Go to Global Card Access [www.bofaml.com/globalcardaccess](https://www.bofaml.com/globalcardaccess%E2%80%8B "www.bofaml.com/globalcardaccess​") and login
- If you have not registered your card on Global Card Access yet, please follow the steps in Obtain your PIN online
- Once logged in, click alerts
- Add your phone number under "contacts" and Click through the settings tab for alerts that you would like to receive.
- The corporate travel card team recommends receiving alerts for "Suspicious Activity", "Transactions Declined", and "Percentage of Credit Limit Used". There are other alerts that can be set up if you are inclined.

**When do I need to use a Corporate Travel & Expense Credit Card?**

[**Workday’s Travel and Expense Policy**](https://horizon.workdayinternal.com/home/ls/content/383002943073927/workday-travel-policy-effective-feb-1-2024 "Workday’s Travel and Expense Policy")

1. Once you have spent $3,000 USD (or local currency equivalent) in annual travel expenses, Workday requires Workmates to request and use a corporate card for all future travel related expenses.
2. Examples of travel expenses are airfare, hotel, lyft/uber, meals while traveling, parking fees, etc.
3. Examples of items that should NOT be charged on your corporate card: cell phone bills, large purchases (over $1,000 USD or local currency equivalent) that can be paid through a purchase order, personal (not business related) expenses, etc.
4. If you’re unsure of whether something should be charged to your corporate card or not, please reach out to expenses@workday.com.

**How to report fraudulent activity on my corporate credit card**

**Call Bank of America’s Fraud Department**

1. North America: (866) 500-8262 or the number on the back of your credit card
2. EMEA & APAC: (877) 436-0960 or the number on the back of your credit card

**If the charge is reported within 30 days of being incurred, they will take over pursuing reimbursement and provide you a new card if the charge is fraudulent in nature.**

1. Submit the credit card transaction in Workday in an expense report under the expense item: “Fraud/Disputed Transaction”

**If the charge is reported after 30 days**

1. Notify the Expense team by emailing expenses@workday.com with the subject line “Fraudulent Credit Card Activity”
2. Reach out to the vendor directly. Depending on the nature of the dispute they may provide a refund to your account.
3. If a refund is not possible notify your manager.
4. Submit the transaction in an expense report under the expense item “Fraud/Disputed Transaction”.

**If a refund is obtained by the vendor**

1. Submit the transaction in an expense report under the expense item “Fraud/Disputed Transaction”.
