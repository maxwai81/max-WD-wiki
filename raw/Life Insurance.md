## What’s in it for me?

Group Life, AD&D, TPD, Critical Illness and Long Term Disability insurances are provided during your employment at Workday. All Workmates on a permanent contract are offered coverage from day one of employment. These benefits are provided by AIA HK, a Zurich partner.

## How does it work?

![Click for full screen](https://benefits.workday.com/assets/img/ui/full-screen.svg)

| Insurer | AIA Hong Kong |
| --- | --- |
| Policy Owner | Workday Asia Pacific Limited |
| Policy Period | 1 February to 31 January |
| Plan Type | - Group Term Life - Group Accidental Death & Disablement (AD&D) - Group Total Permanent Disablement (TPD) - Critical Illness - Long Term Disability |
| Extended Benefits | - Total Disability Extended Death Benefit - Terminal Illness Benefit - Dismemberment Benefit - Simplified Critical Illness Benefit |
| Insured Member Eligibility | - All permanent employees upon commencement of employment & existing active employees - Casuals and contractors are not eligible for cover |
| Eligibility Terms | All permanent employees on commencement of employment and working a minimum of 15 hours per week |
| Age Limit | Entry age of coverage is 64 and Maximum age or renewal up to age 70 |
| Coverage & Evidence Limit | - HK$6,000,000 (Life & TPD) - HK$8,000,000 (AD&D) - HK$ 500,000 (Critical Illness) - waiting period of 60 days - 70% of basic salary (Long Term Disability) - waiting period of 90 days. Medical underwriting may be required.  No Evidence Limit (NEL) means the maximum amount of insurance of an insured as set out in the Insurance Schedule which will be automatically accepted by the insurance company without evidence of insurability, provided that such insured is actively at work on the inception date. Any amount in excess of NEL is subject to undergo Medical Examination if his/her Sum Assured exceeds the above limit. You may be required to go through Medical underwriting to get the full benefit for the Long Term Disability insurance, if this is the case you will be notified accordingly. |
| Coverage Amount | - Term Life 4 x Basic Annual Salary (“BAS”)\* - AD&D 4 x Basic Annual Salary (“BAS”)\* - TPD 4 x Basic Annual Salary (“BAS”)\* - LTD In the event you’re absent from work continuously for 90 days due to illness, our insurance policy may pay up to 70% of your Basic Annual Salary. This Benefit is available for a max of 5 years.  \*BAS means Basic Annual Salary, which is the basic salary excluding overtime, Bonus, allowances, etc |

From 1 April, this benefit will be visible in Workday. Please note that if you joined Workday before 1 April, the coverage start date will still display as 1 April in Workday for visibility purposes only. Your individual coverage date will commence from your hire date. For example, if you joined on 20 February, your coverage will commence from 20 February.
