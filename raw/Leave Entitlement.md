Our comprehensive leave programme helps you take the time to focus on the things that matter most.

## What’s in it for me?

Workday is a fun place to work, with lots of smart, interesting people. Still, you may occasionally need a break from all the awesomeness. That’s why we offer paid public holidays, paid annual leave, and an array of leave options that can help you rest, recharge, and take care of yourself—or your family members.

## PTO policy

To understand the guidelines for booking Annual Leave (PTO), please read the [Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/paid-time-off-policy).

## How do I book my time off?

Log in to Workday and use [Request Absence](https://wd5.myworkday.com/workday/d/inst/13102!CK5mGhIKBggDEMenAhIICgYI1A0Q-yk~*Nw6tDtqihJ0~/cacheable-task/2997$18982.htmld#backheader=true) to view and book your PTO or leave of absence. Ensure your time is correctly logged. If you’ve forgotten to log previous time off, you can log this retroactively.

Watch the video below for more guidance.

## Taking a leave of absence

You should communicate with your People Leader about the timing of your leave and when you expect to return to work. Please communicate as early as possible if your dates or circumstances change.

- Your People Leader is responsible for entering the ' [Return Worker from Leave](https://wd5.myworkday.com/workday/d/task/2997$10383.htmld) ' task in Workday, but Workmates also have the option to return themselves from leave
- Head to this [Workday Knowledge Article](https://wd5.myworkday.com/workday/d/inst/25755$1198/rel-task/2998$40834.htmld), to follow steps on how to return a leave of absence in Workday
