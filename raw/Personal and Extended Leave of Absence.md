Life happens, and we can recognise that there may be times when employees want to take a personal leave or undertake special projects outside of Workday.

## What’s in it for me?

Life happens, and we can recognise that there may be times when employees want to take a personal leave or undertake special projects outside of Workday. Here's some general guidelines to help you along the way.

## Personal Leave of Absence (PLOA)

PLOA may be approved up to a maximum of 90 calendar days.

Eligibility:

● Available globally  
● FTE, Regular and Interns are eligible  
● Available from day 1 of employment

## Extended Personal Leave of Absence

Extended PLOAs may be approved from 91 calendar days up to a maximum of 12 months.

Eligibility:

● \*Employees must have completed, at a minimum, 5 years of service in order to be eligible for consideration of extended leave of absence (Extended PLOA), subject to local legislation  
● The employee requesting leave must be in good performance standing  
● The employee must not have taken a personal leave of absence in the previous two years

\*In France, employees with at least three years of seniority may be eligible to request a Personal Leave of Absence (PLOA), also known as a congé sabbatique, as permitted by French labour law.

## Procedures

An employee who meets the eligibility requirements and wishes to formally request a PLOA or Extended PLOA should discuss this with their direct manager as a first step. If the business is supportive, you should submit the request in Workday, using [Request Absence](https://wd5.myworkday.com/workday/d/inst/13102!CK5mGhIKBggDEMenAhIICgYI1A0Q-yk~*Nw6tDtqihJ0~/cacheable-task/2997$18982.htmld#backheader=true), enter the dates you wish to request the leave, then select Leave of Absence > Personal Leave of Absence or Extended Personal Leave of Absence.

This action will then kick off the approval chain up to SVP for PLOAs and up to SVP and CHRO for Extended PLOAs. Each request will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis and is at business discretion.

If the manager and the employee agree to the conditions above and the request has been approved through the approval chain, the employee will receive a letter outlining the following items which should be reviewed and signed:

- It is an unpaid leave of absence. You will therefore not be entitled to receive any salary, any other cash allowance (i.e. pension contributions) during your leave. The employee’s compensation (base pay plus any incentive compensation) will end on their last day of work
- Most benefits will continue to be funded by Workday if you are currently enrolled and wish to continue, subject to the insurer’s Terms and conditions
- In EMEA, holiday accrual will stop during the leave unless specified by local legislation. For Extended Leave of Absence you are required to use the proportion of your accrued but untaken PTO prior to beginning your leave
- You are required to return all Company property prior to commencement of a Leave of Absence longer than 9 months
- If the employee has unvested stock grants, the stock vesting will continue for 30 days, and will be suspended thereafter until the employee returns to work, at which point vesting will resume
- PLOA and Extended PLOA are unprotected leaves and there is no guarantee that the position the employee is leaving will be available at the end of the leave. Once the time period for the leave is agreed on, it cannot be extended without a written request for extension and approval by management. If it is shortened, prior notice to the direct manager and People and Purpose is also required. The total leave taken may not exceed 12 months or the maximum leave allowable within a country
- It is the responsibility of the employee to connect with their manager at least one month prior to the scheduled return date to discuss employment opportunities and plan for the employee's return
- If you do not wish to return to work following your period of leave then you should ensure that you give proper notice in accordance with your local country

Please be mindful that some Leaves of Absence may be subject to discussion with a People Leader, as we look to support you and the business.

## Do I need to do anything about returning to work?

You should communicate with your People Leader about the timing of your leave and when you expect to return to work. Please communicate as early as possible if your dates or circumstances change.

Your People Leader is responsible for entering the 'Return Worker from Leave' task in Workday, but Workmates also have the option to return themselves from leave.

Head to this [Workday Knowledge Article](https://wd5.myworkday.com/workday/d/inst/25755$1198/rel-task/2998$40834.htmld), to follow steps on how to return a leave of absence in Workday.
