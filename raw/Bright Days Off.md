## What’s in it for me?

Bright Days Off enables Workmates to give back to their communities with up to three days per calendar year to volunteer.

## How does it work?

- The time away can be recorded in Workday as 'Volunteer Time Off' which is exclusively for volunteering purposes
- Bright Days Off do not carry over into the new year; each January, you can use up to three such days during the calendar year
- Once your volunteering is done, log your hours into Benevity
