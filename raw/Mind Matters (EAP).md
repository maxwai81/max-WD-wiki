## What’s in it for me?

Mind Matters, powered by Optum, is Workday’s global mental health programme. It is your trusted resource for holistic mental and emotional health support. All Workmates and their family members can access a comprehensive suite of on-demand resources, unlimited coaching sessions, and 8 sessions of therapy or counselling per person, per year – all at no cost to you. Highly-rated and diverse therapists are available for appointments that can be booked within days, so you never have to wait too long to get support.

**Availability of this benefit is subject to, and only offered in compliance with, the specific local laws, regulations, and healthcare policies of each country.**

## Who is eligible?

All eligible Workmates and their family members (spouses/domestic partners and dependents) will have unlimited coaching sessions and 8 therapy/counselling sessions per person, per year, at no cost. Every family member—including children\*, adults, and couples - can get the care they need.

## What is available to you?

## Accessing Care for Myself or My Family

**Simple, Flexible Access -** Getting support should be easy. Optum EWS offers multiple ways to connect with care so you can choose what works best for you and your loved ones.

**Virtual Care -** Prefer to talk from the comfort of your home? Where available via our Mindfi tool, you can schedule secure video sessions with licensed professionals. Virtual care is convenient, private, and available across time zones. You’ll receive the same high-quality support as an in-person visit, without the need to travel.

**In-Person Sessions -** If face-to-face support feels right for you, Optum EWS can help you find a local provider for short-term counseling. We’ll guide you through the process and make it simple to book an appointment that fits your schedule.

**Phone Support -** Need help right away? Call our 24/7 support line to speak with a counselor or get guidance on work-life resources. Immediate assistance is always available, whether you’re dealing with stress, family concerns, or workplace challenges.

**Support for Household Members -** Your family matters. Household members can access the same confidential services—whether they need counseling, financial advice, or help managing life’s challenges. Optum EWS ensures that support extends beyond the workplace to those closest to you.

**Digital Tools Anytime -** Explore our online platform for self-care programs, stress management exercises, and educational resources. Available 24/7, these tools help you stay proactive about your wellbeing and provide practical strategies for everyday life.

#### What does Optum EWS not cover?

While Optum Employee Wellbeing Services offers a wide range of confidential support, there are certain services and costs that are not included in the program. Here’s what you should know:

**Long-Term or Ongoing Therapy -** Optum EWS provides short-term counseling via up to 8 sessions of support. This is designed to help you address immediate concerns and develop coping strategies. If you require long-term or specialized therapy, we can help connect you to external providers, but ongoing care may not be covered under EWS.

**Medical or Psychiatric Treatment -** EWS does not replace medical care or psychiatric treatment. If you need medication management or clinical interventions, you’ll be referred to appropriate healthcare professionals outside the EWS program.

**Inpatient or Residential Programs -** Services such as inpatient hospitalization, residential treatment, or intensive outpatient programs are not included. Optum EWS can assist with referrals and guidance to help you find suitable options.

**Legal Representation -** While EWS offers legal consultations and guidance for common issues, it does not cover attorney fees or representation in court. You’ll receive advice and resources, but any extended legal services are your responsibility.

**Financial Payments or Debt Settlement -** EWS provides financial planning tools and guidance, but it does not offer direct financial assistance, debt repayment, or loans.

**Important Note: Optum EWS is designed to provide short-term, solution-focused support and connect you with resources for ongoing care when needed. Always check with your health plan or benefits provider for coverage details on extended services.**

## Support for Children Under 16 and Under 12

Optum Employee Wellbeing Services (EWS) recognizes that children and adolescents have unique needs when it comes to emotional and mental wellbeing. Here’s how we provide age-appropriate support:

**Children Under 16**

- **Specialized Counselling:** Optum EWS offers access to licensed professionals experienced in working with teens and adolescents. Sessions focus on building coping skills, managing stress, and addressing school or social challenges.
- **Parental Involvement:**For minors, parents or guardians are included in the process to ensure a supportive environment and continuity of care.
- **Flexible Options:** Counseling can be provided virtually or in person, depending on availability and family preference.

**Children Under 12**

- **Family-Centered Approach:** For younger children, Optum EWS emphasizes family participation. Sessions often involve parents or caregivers to help create strategies that support the child’s emotional health.
- **Guidance for Parents:**Resources and coaching for parents to manage behavioral concerns and foster a positive home environment.

**Important Notes**

**Age of Majority:** The age of majority in each country and also the rules surrounding child and minor support determine what support we are allowed to offer via the EWS. Please call in to discuss what support is available in each country and to receive guidance.  
**Consent Requirements:** Parental or guardian consent is required for all services involving minors.  
**Scope of Care:** Optum EWS provides short-term, solution-focused support. If ongoing or specialized care is needed, we’ll assist with referrals to appropriate providers.  
**Confidentiality:** While parents are involved, sessions maintain a respectful balance of privacy for the child.
