## What’s in it for me?

We ask Workmates to avoid carrying out any business trip on weekends and public holidays. Nevertheless, we understand that in certain situations, it is impossible not to travel during weekends and public holidays.

Therefore, you can benefit of Time Off in Lieu.

## How does it work?

**Weekend/Public Holiday Business Travel Time Off In Lieu (TOIL)**

- All requests to travel for business outside of normal working time must be initiated by your People Leader in order to qualify for Time Off In Lieu (TOIL). Where possible, we encourage all Workmates to travel during their normal working hours. If workmates are required to travel on weekends and/or public holidays, they must only travel and they are not permitted to work.
- As an example, if Workmates are travelling on a weekend: Saturday - working time as travelling = TOIL. Sunday - non-work day/rest day = non-TOIL.
- Travel on a weekend and/or public holiday must be requested, or approved, by email in advance by the Workmate’s People Leader. The workmate must make it clear to their People Leader that the dates of travel include a weekend and/or public holiday and TOIL will be taken.
- Workmates are subsequently responsible for booking TOIL for Business Travel into Workday and should aim to use this within six months of the return travel date, we encourage you to use it as soon as practical. Any outstanding TOIL for Business Travel is not payable upon leaving Workday and therefore must be used prior.

**TOIL available for weekend and/or public holidays business travel as follows:**

- Travel in region on weekend/public holiday: **0.5 day**
- Travel outside of region or 8 hours+ travel time on the weekend/public holiday: **1 day**

**Use of policy:**

- Workday anticipates that using Time Off In Lieu (TOIL) will generally be needed for no more than 8 days within a single calendar year
- If you have concerns about your upcoming workload, you should schedule a meeting with your People Leader to discuss it
