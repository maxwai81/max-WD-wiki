## What’s in it for me?

We provide Workmates with PTO to support your work-life balance and wellbeing. You’re required to accurately record all PTO taken in Workday. It is important to utilise your PTO and Workday reserves the right to manage PTO balances to ensure compliance with this policy.

This policy applies to all full-time, permanent employees in EMEA, APAC & Japan.

## How does it work?

**PTO accrual and recording**

- Workmates must accurately record all PTO taken in Workday
- A minimum of 20 days of PTO per calendar year (1 January - 31 December) must be taken by each Workmate
- Annual leave carryover may be permitted, subject to a deadline date. Refer to your [local entitlement policy](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/annual-leave)

**Managing PTO balances**

- The business may require Workmates and their People Leaders to schedule leave to reduce excessive PTO balances or to meet the minimum annual PTO target
- If we suspect a discrepancy in a Workmate’s PTO balance, an audit will be conducted in collaboration with the Workmate and their People Leader
- Any unutilised accrued PTO will be forfeited, with the exception of Australia and New Zealand where local laws apply
- Potential encashment of PTO may be subject to delay or withholding pending the outcome of an audit, should your PTO balance be deemed inaccurate. Refer to your [local entitlement policy](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/leave-entitlement) for further details

**PTO during notice period**

- Workmates are expected to exhaust their remaining PTO balance during their notice period
- Your People Leader may exercise discretion to balance the needs of the business with your request, regarding the timing of PTO during the notice period

**Sick Leave during PTO**

- If a Workmate becomes ill while on PTO, they must immediately contact their People Leader
- Upon notification and providing appropriate documentation (please refer to your local sick leave policy), the PTO days will be canceled and converted to Sick Leave

**Fair Usage and Guardrails**

Minimum and maximum leave notice:

- Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for requests up to 3 working days, and 4 weeks for longer absences
- Emergency leave may be considered with shorter notice but must be justified and approved by a line manager
- Leave is not approved until confirmed in Workday

Peak period restrictions:

- During key business periods (e.g., fiscal year-end, major product launches, Workday Rising events), team-based leave caps may be enforced
- Managers will communicate blackout periods in advance and apply fair rotation principles for approving leave

Leave spreading requirement:

- A minimum of 10 days annual leave must be used by 30 September to promote wellness
- People & Purpose will conduct quarterly monitoring and issue reminders to employees with low usage

Prohibition on excessive carry-over:

- Standard carry-over is limited to 5 days\*. Extensions are considered only for statutory reasons or approved business cases
- Extended carry-over requires documented People Business Partner approval

\* *Note: While our standard guideline for carry-over is limited to 5 days, our policy on leave carry-over is subject to and will be managed in full compliance with local statutory regulations. In regions where a different carry-over period is mandated by law, the local requirement will apply.*

Return-to-back-to-back leave prohibition:

- Combining annual and sick leave (e.g., sick leave directly before/after holiday) may trigger a review
- Repeated patterns without proper documentation will prompt formal P&P investigation

## Responsibilities

Workmates

- Accurately record all PTO taken in Workday
- Utilise a minimum of 20 days of PTO per year
- Do not carry-over more than 5 days
- Cooperate with the business during PTO balance audits
- Inform your People Leader immediately if they become ill during PTO

People Leaders

## Cooperation

Repeated or deliberate breach of policy, including misuse of sick leave, failure to follow booking protocols, or abuse of carry-over entitlements, may result in formal disciplinary action.

## Review and updates

This policy will be reviewed and updated periodically, or as needed, to ensure its effectiveness and compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
