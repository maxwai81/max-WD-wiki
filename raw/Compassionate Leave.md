## What’s in it for me?

Workday supports employees through times of difficulty, with a global Compassionate Leave policy that allows for up to 10 days of paid leave for challenging events and circumstances.

## How does it work?

Compassionate Leave is intended to help Workmates when the unexpected happens, and it is not possible to work while balancing your health, safety, and the needs of your loved ones.

- Workmates may take up to 10 days per cause or event (see the next bullet). If more time is required, you should talk to your People Leader to explore your options, including time covered under other leave policies.
- We define an event as a **standalone**, **unanticipated** life event that is **not already covered** under other Workday leave programmes or policies, such as sick leave. (A circumstance involving multiple bereavements is an exception to discuss with your people leader.)
- If you have any questions as to whether your need is covered by the compassionate leave policy or a different policy, please submit a [People Guide Request](https://wd5.myworkday.com/workday/email-universal/task/2997$18295.htmld).
- Workmates in the **United States** please visit the [local information on Horizon](https://horizon.workdayinternal.com/a/workday/home/ls/content/compassionate-leave).

## You can take compassionate leave for the following events:

- Death of your loved one, including a friend, family member or pet
- Miscarriage loss at any stage of pregnancy or stillbirth (available to both parents)
- Reproductive health treatments (available to both parents)
- Menopause, for time to recharge and any associated issues
- Menstruation, when experiencing severe symptoms such as painful cramps, dizziness, migraines, diarrhoea, nausea, and vomiting associated with menstrual periods which create significant difficulties in performing your role
- Divorce or separation from a domestic partner, time to process the emotional impact
- Domestic abuse, physical or sexual assault, to enable you to seek the support you need
- Home emergency, such as burglary, fire or flood
- Caregiving, such as illness, injury or special needs of a dependent or elderly family member, short term foster care, or unplanned school closures where children need support to learn at home
- Supporting caregivers, so you can give a helping hand to caregivers close to you
- Travel related to adoption, caregiving needs, funeral attendance, or medical treatment for yourself or as a companion
- Donation such as blood or bone marrow
- Emergency related to war, military conflict, and risks to health and safety of your loved ones

## Application and notification

- Workmates should notify their People Leader as soon as possible of the need for Compassionate Leave
- While immediate notification is prioritised, we understand that in certain circumstances, notification may be delayed

## Observation

While we aim to provide compassionate support, in cases of suspected abuse or excessive use of Compassionate Leave, Workmates may be required to provide reasonable evidence to support their leave request.

- We would expect that Compassionate Leave will be utilised no more than once or twice per year, per Workmate (totalling no more than 10 days inclusive)
- We reserve the right to review and potentially modify or remove this benefit if it is found to be consistently abused

## Additional resources: Counselling and relief

Sometimes things can’t be resolved in a matter of days. You and your family have access to professional counseling services through [Mind Matters](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/eap), for help through life’s changes and challenges, large and small.

Workmates may also apply for help through our expanded [Employee Relief Fund](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/employee-relief-fund) programme when experiencing unexpected events like the death of a family member, domestic or physical abuse and support for those who have family displaced by wartime events. Visit [E4E Relief](https://workday.e4erelief.org/PBD-001021/Home/IndexNa?fn=PAG-001258&lang=1033) for further information and to apply.
