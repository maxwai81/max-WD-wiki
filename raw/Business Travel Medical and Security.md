When you’re travelling for business - or travelling anywhere, really - things can happen. We’ve got your back.

## What’s in it for me?

You may need advice ahead of your trip about a country’s security. You may have a medical emergency and need immediate help. Or maybe you misplaced or lost your medication or travel documents. With International SOS (ISOS) at the ready, you can travel confidently and safely.

## How does it work?

Workday has a 24/7 resource on call, online, and on the ground. We’ll help with any of your medical, security or logistical questions, concerns and situations that may arise.

So, if you need:

- Pre-travel advice
- A medical referral
- Information about lost or missing medication or travel documents
- Help navigating a medical or security crisis

Workday has a fully integrated programme in place so you receive the care and expertise that you need, whenever and wherever you need it.

How International SOS can support you

The Duty of Care programme is designed primarily to provide medical and security assistance to employees who are travelling on business outside their home/payroll country:

| **Outside Home Country** | **Inside Home Country** |
| --- | --- |
| **Travel Assistance: ISOS** - call for advice, referral, management of medical and security cases, evacuations/repatriations | **Travel Assistance: ISOS** - you can view the ISOS app for information, but full service not available while in your home country |
| **Business Travel Accident: Chubb -** coverage for evacuation/repatriation as approved by ISOS (if more than 100 miles from home) and Accidental Death and Dismemberment (AD&D) benefits and personal goods (baggage delay, lost luggage, lost or damaged personal items, financial instruments) coverage while on business travel | **Business Travel Accident: Chubb** - coverage for evacuation/repatriation as approved by ISOS (if more than 100 miles from home) and Accidental Death and Dismemberment (AD&D) benefits and personal goods (baggage delay, lost luggage, lost or damaged personal items, financial instruments) coverage while on business travel |
| **Business Travel Medical: Chubb -** coverage for urgent and emergent/unexpected medical care for employees travelling outside of their home country on business | **Business Travel Medical:** Please use your local/home country medical insurance for any illness or injury while travelling for business |

## Before you travel

- First things first: [Download the ISOS app](https://www.internationalsos.com/assistance-app) and sign up with your Workday email.
- Book all travel (flights, hotels, car rentals) with [Egencia](https://horizon.workdayinternal.com/home/egencia-contact-information).
- If you’ve booked travel in Egencia to a destination with elevated risk, you will receive a pre-travel notice email from ISOS. Review and make any needed accommodations.
- Need a visa for travel, or not sure if you do? Head [here](https://horizon.workdayinternal.com/home/cibt-visa-service) for further help.

## During your travel

When you travel out of the country for work with Workday, you have additional insurance coverage for emergency and urgent medical care up to $500,000 USD; emergency dental (unlimited), travel accident, death, and dismemberment; and lost or stolen personal property (up to $5,000 USD).

**For any and all assistance needed during travel, contact ISOS** at your [nearest ISOS assistance centre](https://www.internationalsos.com/locations) (also available via the call button on your ISOS app).

ISOS is available 24/7 for travel-related help and will connect you with the right insurance partner as needed. Scenarios where ISOS can help include:

- Personal illness or injury
- Access to medical advice or to speak with a doctor
- Loss of prescription medication or travel documents
- Lost or stolen personal items
- Personal safety & security concerns (for real-time emergencies, contact local emergency lines and [Workday Workplace Safety](https://horizon.workdayinternal.com/home/ls/content/7843366817997917/globalworkplacesafety) first)
- Direct payment for covered benefits

Our Benefits Overview [Chubb](https://benefits.workday.com/file/27d76cefa405c5a0652fd27ab1bfcd678215b43d?i=1) document provides an overview of the coverage you have available.

For more information check-out our [Global Workday Travel Policy](https://horizon.workdayinternal.com/home/ls/content/383002943073927/workday-travel-policy-effective-feb-1-2024) and the [Workday Expense and Corporate Card Policy](https://horizon.workdayinternal.com/home/ls/content/3025275596469865/workday-expense-and-corporate-card-policy).

## Important information about business travel to high-risk areas

**Restricted Coverage:**

Please be aware that standard BTA coverage is limited for business travel to the following locations:

- Afghanistan
- Iraq
- Russia
- Ukraine
- Israel
- Gaza
- West Bank
- Lebanon

**Before you travel:**

If you are planning business travel to any of these high-risk areas, you must contact [Global Workplace Safety](mailto:safety@workday.com) before booking your trip. This is necessary to:

1. Evaluate the purpose of your travel.
2. Determine if supplemental Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D) insurance from Chubb is required. This will ensure you have full coverage during your trip.
3. Once you have been granted permission to travel by the Global Workplace Safety team, please raise a [People Guide Request](https://wd5.myworkday.com/workday/email-universal/task/2997$18295.htmld) so that the Benefits team can arranage travel insurance on your behalf. Please note the insurance is subject to security approval.

**Safety and security:**

When traveling to a high-risk destination:

- You will receive a mandatory briefing from Global Workplace Safety about the specific risks involved
- You will be required to follow certain safety and security measures, which may include additional protocols and restrictions
- In most cases, these measures will require approval from a VP or higher
- Any extra travel expenses incurred due to these measures will be charged to your department's cost center

**Important consequences:**

Failure to comply with the required safety and security measures can result in:

- Immediate cancellation of your trip
- Forced return to your point of origin
- Disciplinary action, potentially including termination of employment
- Please prioritise your safety and adhere to all guidelines provided by Global Workplace Safety

**Does our travel medical programme cover my personal travel?**

Workday’s travel insurance does not cover you during personal travel unless it is in conjunction with business travel, up to 14 days before, during, or at the end of your business trip. The Chubb Business Travel Accident (BTA) policy, which covers emergency out of country travel medical expenses, evacuation, repatriation, and accidental death and dismemberment:

- Full-time active employees and their accompanying dependent spouse or domestic partner and dependent children (up to age 26 years)  
	Interns, prospective employees, and guests.

**What is covered for domestic travellers?**

Workmates are covered under the Chubb Business Travel Accident (BTA) policy for:

- Accidental Death & Dismemberment
- Evacuation and Repatriation coverage is available if you have travelled for business over 100 miles from your normal permanent place of residence
- Lost baggage (replacement cost of clothes, personal hygiene items) $5,000 USD maximum, $500 USD per item
- Personal property (lost/damaged property) $2,500 USD per trip, $500 USD per item

**Can International SOS provide me with pre-trip information on vaccinations and medical conditions for the countries I plan to visit?**

Yes, you can login to [International SOS.com](https://www.internationalsos.com/MasterPortal/default.aspx?membnum=11BCPH829218) with your membership number (**11BCPH829218**) or find information via the [ISOS assistance app](https://www.internationalsos.com/assistance-app) on vaccination requirements, infectious diseases common in destinations, food and water safety, and tips on staying healthy while abroad. You can also [call an assistance centre](https://www.internationalsos.com/locations) for vaccination and medical advice on countries around the world.

**Can I arrange a service myself and send the bill to ISOS for reimbursement?**

All assistance services should be arranged and coordinated by ISOS to guarantee direct payment.

**My Workday healthcare plan includes coverage for travel. If I need to file a claim, which insurance should I use first?**

Always start with ISOS when you are travelling and need to file a claim.

**I didn’t take advantage of the direct pay arrangement with ISOS and have an eligible medical bill to claim back. How do I file a claim and what is the expected turnaround time?**

If you did not call ISOS at the time that you needed medical assistance and need to file for reimbursement, follow the directions under "How to make a claim with Chubb". Chubb processes most reimbursement claims within 10-14 business days (excluding those that may require additional information or details).

**What documentation do I need to file a claim for a medically necessary quarantine?**

- Completed Claim Form (ISOS will provide this to you)
- Letter from medical provider (Call ISOS for physician recommendation) or government agency showing medical forced quarantined
- Positive clincial test result
- Reasonable expenses incurred for lodging and meals
- The cost of a one-way Economy Airfare ticket to either the Covered Person’s Home Country or to rejoin the group; and Non-refundable travel arrangements.

**I have a chronic health condition and am receiving regular medical support. Which coverage applies when I am travelling for work?**

Workmates with a chronic health condition should reach out to ISOS as well as their treating physician ahead of their trip. Medical professionals will be able to advise how best to manage the condition while travelling. ISOS will assist in filing a claim for any unexpected events. For routine or expected care needs, please file a claim with your local healthcare plan.

**Are pre-existing conditions excluded under medical evacuation service?**

No. International SOS coverage includes evacuation service for pre-existing conditions.

**I need to see a doctor, but I don’t know who is reputable or provides the services I need.**

ISOS has a network of trusted and verified doctors, hospitals, and clinics throughout the globe. Give ISOS a call for assistance finding your closest support.

**I feel ill or I've been injured and need to go to a hospital, but I’m in another country and I’m not sure if my insurance will work.**

ISOS works directly with Workday’s Business Travel Accident insurance partner, Chubb. Call your nearest International [SOS assistance centre](https://www.internationalsos.com/locations) or use the International SOS mobile app to dial the nearest centre and they’ll provide assistance in coordinating your claim.

**Can I talk to an English-speaking doctor at ISOS?**

Yes. ISOS members can speak to coordinating doctors who are licensed from the U.S.A., Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, France and other European countries about any medical issue. The coordinating doctors have extensive experience in emergency medicine, travel medicine and the medical conditions in their respective regions.

**What if I lose my prescription medication?**

Where legally permissible, ISOS can help obtain replacements. Workmates are responsible for the cost of the items and shipping & handling charges.

**Can I go to the dentist for a cleaning on my trip?**

The plan does not cover routine dental services. If you are travelling and accidentally damage a tooth or experience pain in a tooth the plan will cover to alleviate the pain to a manageable level so it can be treated upon your return.

**I am planning to take a personal vacation directly before/after my business meeting abroad, will I still be insured?**

Yes, however this does not extend to trip cancellation coverage. The Workday Business Travel Accident insurance policy covers personal travel undertaken in conjunction with a business trip. There is no limit on the number of days of sojourn/personal deviation however personal travel independent of a business trip would not be covered. As such, all ISOS services would be available to you during personal travel directly connected with business travel.

Workmates can consider purchasing travel insurance for trip cancellation through a third party provider such as Allianz ([www.allianztravelinsurance.com](http://www.allianztravelinsurance.com/)) or Travel Guard ([www.travelguard.com](https://www.travelguard.com/)) If you are booking a flight or train check with that specific company to see if they offer Trip Insurance as well.
