## Our Mental Health Partner: Mind Matters

Whatever is on your mind, getting access to high-quality, on-demand mental and emotional health support just got easier.

[Mind Matters](https://benefits.workday.com/eap) helps you get comprehensive, confidential care for your emotional and mental health - however, whenever, and wherever you need it.

All Workmates and their family members now have access, at no cost, to a comprehensive suite of on-demand resources, unlimited coaching sessions, and 8 sessions of therapy or counselling per person per year—through our new provider, Optum\* (\*121help in India, Vhi in Ireland). Highly-rated and diverse therapists are available for appointments that can be booked within days, so you never have to wait long to get support.

## Dedicated Workplace Mental Health Consultants

Our full-time, dedicated Workplace Mental Health Consultants (WMHCs) are available to support:

- Workmates with unlimited virtual or in-person therapy sessions. This support is limited to the country in which the WMHC is licensed to practice. However, WMHCs can support all Workmates, regardless of location, with helpful consultations that address their needs.
- Workmates seeking couples/family counselling can receive support through convenient virtual sessions, if clinically appropriate.\*
- Workmates’ dependents (children) can receive support through virtual sessions, if clinically appropriate.

\*For family, group, or couples sessions, Workmates must indicate to the WMHC that they wish to include other individuals before the first session is completed.

## How do I access the services?

To access services, log-in to the digital platform at https://supportfinder.optum.com/workday.

For more information, head to our dedicated [Mind Matters](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/eap) page.

If you’ve got a question our People Guide team is here to help! Simply submit a [People Guide Request](https://workday.service-now.com/servicehub?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=50303249dbcd3f00b89d7e88f4961948&sysparm_category=e15706fc0a0a0aa7007fc21e1ab70c2f).
