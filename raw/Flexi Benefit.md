We're delighted to offer all Workmates in Hong Kong access to the Flexi Benefit Programme.

This benefit gives you the flexibility to choose a range of benefits to be expensed from your annual entitlement and is in complement to the corporate medical plan with AIA.

## What's in it for me?

You will receive the new reset amount from 1 February each year for 12 months:

- Employee – HKD 10,000
- Employee + Partner – HKD 12,000
- Employee + Children – HKD 15,000
- Employee + Partner + Children –HKD 15,000

Your wellbeing is important to us! The Flexi Benefit programme is designed to give you the power to choose the benefits that best meet your individual needs. To help you maximise your Flexi Benefit, we're introducing a cap on the holiday subsidy category.

- Employees can expense up to 50% of their total annual holiday entitlement through the Flexi Benefit
- This change encourages you to explore the wide range of other valuable benefits available through the programme, such as Maternity Expenses, Chiropractic / Homeopathy, Health Screenings and more

## How does it work?

It takes only simple 3 steps:

1. Download the TribeGo app (note only available via mobile app)
2. Register & scan the QR code (for first timer!) OR Enter user ID and password
3. Start your claim

## Reimbursement Schedule

Your claim submitted will be reimbursed via regular payroll. The claim submitted by every 28th of the month, will be paid in the next payroll cycle.

The last claim submission cut off: by the last day of February every year. If you fail to submit by this date, the balance of the Flexi amount will not be carried over to the next policy year and your claim will not be processed and reimbursed.

## Enrolment

Auto enrollment and the flexi amount allocation available on the first of the following month after joining. The allocation will be prorated for the year.

If you experience any of the below life events your entitlement will be changed. Please be reminded that the entitlement is given based on the cover category enrolled in the Corporate Medical plan.

## Who is eligible?

Applicable to full-time and permanent employees. Prorated by quarter for new hires.
