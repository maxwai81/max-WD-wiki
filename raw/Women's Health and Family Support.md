If you’re seeking fertility treatment, expecting, or just had a baby we’ve 24/7 support from Maven! Maven, our premiere family benefit, is your personal guide to parenthood.

## What's in it for me?

You can begin using Maven at any stage in your fertility or parenting journey, with continuous support through to your child's tenth birthday.

Maven has plenty of support for male members too, check out our article for more [information](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/article/supporting-dads-at-every-stage).

**Availability of the Benefit is subject to, and only offered in compliance with, the specific local laws, regulations, and healthcare policies of each country.**

## How does it work?

### Use Maven for:

#### On-demand virtual appointments with top-rated practitioners at no cost and no co-pay.

Unlimited messaging and on-demand video appointments with Maven OB-GYNs, mental health specialists, prenatal nutritionists, lactation consultants, career coaches, and many others from wherever you are, whenever you need it.

#### Designated Care Advocates for personalised referrals

Personalised, one-on-one support to help answer questions about your benefits, get connected to the right Maven providers for your needs, and for referrals to in-person care when necessary.

#### Free virtual live classes and a library of expert content, tailored to your journey

Clinically informed content tailored to you on topics like prenatal health, postpartum depression, and returning to work with confidence.

#### Reimbursement support for family building expenses

Workday will reimburse each family unit through Maven Wallet\* for eligible egg/sperm freezing, IVF/IUI treatments, adoption or surrogacy expenses. This is subject to a maximum lifetime amount related to cost of care in your location.

\* *Any reimbursement you receive through Maven Wallet is intended to be treated as taxable income to you*.

## Workday's comprehensive Maven offering includes:

**Maven:** All Workmates have access to Maven’s on-demand suite of virtual support, content, and community for every stage of family planning.

**Maven Wallet:** Reimbursement\* for adoption, fertility, and surrogacy expenses. This is subject to a maximum lifetime amount related to cost of care in your location.

**Maven Menopause:** A [specialised support programme](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/menopause-support) for every stage of the menopause journey.

**Maven Milk:** To help ease the transition back to work travel for breastfeeding parents, Maven provides the gear to help you safely store and travel with milk. Plus, [lactation consultants](https://benefits.workday.com/file/6ec5c2cfabbf6104938929a3445bc1b6d48e6ec2?i=1) are now available to help you reach your breastfeeding goals. See an overview of the [US programme](https://benefits.workday.com/file/6b2b8a2763131eb2444dafe43728a73c7fc76194?i=1) and [International Programme](https://benefits.workday.com/file/39682aeba92a61a848784d2cc4a78b62e4d3b88e?i=1).

**Maven Parenting & Pediatrics:** An expansion of the original Maven virtual programme, Workmates now have access to Maven paediatricians and children’s behavioural care support through a child’s tenth birthday.

**Maven Wellness:** A [specialised programme](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/period-health-support) that provides support on topics like Endometriosis, PCOS, Irregular Periods, Birth Control, Nutrition and Career Coaching.

*\*Any reimbursement you receive through Maven Wallet is intended to be treated as taxable income to you.*

## What does it cost?

Workday employees, their partners and dependent children (up to age 26) receive unlimited resources, appointments and messaging through Maven at no cost.

Please note that any reimbursement you receive through Maven Wallet is intended to be treated as taxable income to you.

## How to access this benefit

Head [here](https://www.mavenclinic.com/maven-enrollment/maven?organization_id=61&install_source=enterprise&install_content=intranet&install_campaign=2019_workday_intranet) or download the Maven Clinic app on iOS or Android. For help, contact [support@mavenclinic.com](mailto:support@mavenclinic.com).

Enrolment is **confidential** and will not be shared with Workday.

**New Joiners** can enrol anytime after your first day and you'll will be able to access the services after your first week at Workday.
