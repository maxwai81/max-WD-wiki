## What’s in it for me?

Every employee here has the opportunity to become a Workday shareholder, because all are eligible for stock grants and also a discount when purchasing Workday stock. To help you prepare for retirement, our pension can help you meet your savings goals. And to help you prepare for the future, you’ll have access to lots of ways to invest.

## How does it work?

![Click for full screen](https://benefits.workday.com/assets/img/ui/full-screen.svg)

|  | ESPP\* | Mandatory Pension Plan\* | Voluntarily Pension Plan\* | Rental Reimbursement Program |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Description** | [Monthly savings scheme](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/stock-and-espp) to purchase shares every 6 months | [Mandatory scheme to provide for retirement savings](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/mandatory-pension) | [Voluntary scheme to save tax free](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/voluntary-pension) | [Tax free rental reimbursement scheme](https://benefits.workday.com/hk/house-rent-reimbursement) |
| **Company Contribution** | Up to 15% discount | 5% of Relevant Income | 5% of Basic Salary after mandatory contribution | N/A |
| **Employee Contribution** | Up to 15% of salary | 5% of Relevant Income | N/A | 40% of basic salary |
| **Tax Free** | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Timeline for Investments** | 6 months | Until Retirement | Until Retirement | Monthly |
| **Earliest Funds can be accessed** | 6 months | 65 | 65 | Monthly |
| **What happens if you leave** | Savings Returned | Immediate vesting | Immediate vesting | Scheme stops |
| **Mandatory/Voluntary participation** | Voluntary | Mandatory | Voluntary | Voluntary |
| **Default Provider** | Workday | HSBC Hong Kong | HSBC Hong Kong | Employee Self Funded |

\*The value of investments can go down as well as up, so you may get back less than you invest.
